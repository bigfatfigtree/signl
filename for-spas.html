How it works

How SIGNL works here

/ 01

Offer a quiet-hour treatment

Comp a massage, a float session or a facial in the slots that sit empty: the Tuesday afternoon, the early weekday morning. You set the treatment, its ceiling and how many visits you want this month. The comp costs you your cost, not its menu price.

/ 02

Creators visit like real guests

Local creators claim the offer, book through the app and redeem the comp inside the app on a single-use code. Your register and your booking system are never touched, and the visit reads like any other appointment on your book.

/ 03

The post lands in 48 hours

A tracked Standard Visit about the real visit: 1 post or reel plus 1 story that shares the post: a personal experience, honestly told, fully disclosed, with no treatment claims. If a post misses the window, the visit is replaced and does not count against your plan.

Honest FAQ

Questions, answered straight

Does influencer marketing work for spas and massage studios?

Yes, because spa bookings run on trust and word of mouth, and a treatment is a natural comp. Local creators visit like real guests in the hours you choose, post honestly within 48 hours, and their followers are people who can actually book a table this week: people who live close enough to come back monthly.

What does the comp actually cost my spa?

Whatever treatment you decide to offer: a 60-minute massage, a float, a facial. You set the comp and its ceiling on every offer, and creators opt in knowing exactly what it is. The comp costs you your cost, not its menu price: your therapist's time in a slot that would otherwise sit empty. The comp is your own service, provided by you, and SIGNL never touches comp money: no pass-through, no markup, and no per-creator cash fees on standard visits.

Can creators make claims about what a treatment does?

No. Creator posts about spas and wellness services are personal experiences only: what the visit was like, never claims about results, healing or health outcomes. Every post carries full disclosure that the visit was comped, and your practice stays responsible for its own advertising rules. That restraint is what keeps the posts believable.

How much does it cost?

Published pricing: $299 a month for Self-Service (up to 8 creator visits a month, about $37 per creator visit), $599 a month for Growth (up to 20 creator visits a month, about $30 per creator visit) or $999 a month for Managed (up to 30 creator visits a month), with a 3-month minimum, then month to month. Your only other cost is the comp you choose to offer, provided at your cost, not retail. There are no per-creator cash fees on standard visits.

What is the Visit Guarantee?

If a booked creator does not show up or does not post, that visit does not count against your monthly visits. It is replaced.

What is the refund policy?

Paid bookings are held by SIGNL until the post is live. If the visit does not happen or the post never lands, the business is refunded in full automatically. If we cannot deliver the service, including a founding market that does not open, founding businesses receive a full refund of what they paid for that market. Plans run month to month after the 3 month minimum. Cancelling stops future billing. Visits already delivered are not refundable.
