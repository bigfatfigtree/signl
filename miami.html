In Short

What SIGNL is: a local creator marketing platform. Local businesses comp local creators a meal, service or experience, and creators post honestly about it: 1 post or reel plus 1 story that shares the post, inside a 48-hour standard.

Status in Miami: pre-launch, securing founding creators across Miami-Dade now. Creators join free and are reviewed in sign-up order. Businesses sign up when we are ready to launch: they join the founding list today and are charged nothing until the market opens.

Miami posts in Spanish: two of every three Miami-Dade residents speak Spanish at home (U.S. Census Bureau). The claim and profile setup run in Spanish, this page has a full Spanish edition, and a Miami profile can carry a Spanish-speaking audience percentage next to its local reach.

Two ways creators earn: comped visits, where the meal, service or experience is the pay, and paid bookings at the creator's own rate, from $50, where the creator keeps 97 percent.

How creators are ranked: by local reach, the share of their audience that lives in Miami-Dade, shown ahead of follower count on every profile.

Founding pricing for businesses: $199 a month for Self-Service, $349 for Growth and $699 for Managed, with $100 off the first month, a 3-month minimum, and the founding rate held while the plan stays active.

For creators: the deal, up front

SIGNL is free for creators. Always. Businesses pay the platform. You never pay to join, to list, or to get booked.

Comp visits are the core. Paid bookings sit on top: set your own rate, the business pays it, and the platform fee is 6 percent total, split evenly, so you keep 97 percent of your rate. Paid bookings are held by SIGNL until your post is live. Headliners (15,000+ followers) are also first in line for paid hero campaigns when businesses run them.

The numbers that matter

Local reach beats follower count here

Miami follower counts mix neighbors, tourists and half of Latin America, and they cannot tell them apart. SIGNL can. Every creator here is ranked by local reach: the share of their audience that actually lives in Miami-Dade.

That is the headline number on every Miami profile, ahead of follower count, and Miami profiles carry a second number next to it: the Spanish-speaking share of the audience. A Little Havana creator with 5,000 followers, most of them in Miami and most of them Spanish-speaking, is worth more to a Calle Ocho restaurant than a 200,000-follower travel account whose audience lands at the airport twice a year. Businesses browsing the roster see both shares first, and they can filter the roster for Spanish-speaking creators.

It cuts both ways, and both lanes are open from the first breath: take a comped visit, a meal, service or experience for the standard post and story, or be booked at your own listed rate and keep 97 percent. The comp rules, read these

Simple deal, enforced fairly

The comp is the pay

A free meal or service is your compensation. Creators are never on a payroll, businesses supply the comp, you supply the post.

48-hour posting standard

48 hours is the standard: your post should be live within 48 hours of the visit, and on-time posts earn Momentum. A post inside a week still counts. Missing the week, or going silent, is what costs roster standing.

FTC disclosure is required

Every post must clearly disclose the comp (e.g. #gifted / "meal provided by…"). No exceptions, it protects you and the business.

No-shows lose their roster spot

Claim it, show up. A no-show without notice, or a visit with no post, removes you from the roster. Businesses plan around your visit.

Honest posts only

Nobody can require a positive review. Post your real take. Businesses that pressure creators get removed, your credibility is the whole product.

/ 02

Founding pricing, held for you

$199 a month for Self-Service (up to 5 creator visits a month, about $40 per creator visit), $349 a month for Growth (up to 10 creator visits a month, about $35 per creator visit) or $699 a month for Managed (up to 20 creator visits a month), with a 3-month minimum and then month to month. Founding businesses get $100 off the first month. Visits you don't use roll into the next month, then expire. The only other cost is the comp the business chooses to offer, provided at its cost, not retail. Founding businesses keep founding pricing while they remain subscribed, and the plan begins when the Miami market opens.

/ 03

Activate when Miami opens

Pick creators from the founding roster, filter by local reach and Spanish-speaking audience, offer a comp at your cost, and get honest, tracked posts delivered in 48 hours* of each visit.

*Creators have up to one week to post. 48 hours is the standard they are held to, and hitting it earns their Momentum. If a week passes with no post, the visit is replaced and does not count against your plan.

The Visit Guarantee

If a booked creator does not show up or does not post, that visit does not count against your monthly visits. It is replaced.

How content rights work: on a comp visit, you can share the creator's post natively with the creator credited, and nothing more: no downloads, no reposting the file, no ads. On a paid visit, you can also repost the content on your own channels and run it as an ad for 90 days under the standard license included in the booking. The creator always owns the content. Rights follow the visit, never the plan.
