Momentum: how posting on time gets you booked more

If you want to know how to get more brand deals as a small creator, start with the boring part that actually decides it. Businesses rebook creators who post when they said they would. Momentum is how Magpie makes that record visible, so reliability stops being a private impression and starts working for you on your profile.

By Jeff, Founder, Magpie · October 26, 2026 · Magpie Learn hub

The short answer

What is Momentum, in plain words?

Momentum points are Magpie's reliability signal for creators. You earn them by posting on time after a visit, and they raise your visibility to local businesses. They sit alongside local reach on your profile, so a business can see both how local your audience is and how reliably you deliver.

That pairing matters. Local reach gets you considered. Momentum helps you get chosen again. Magpie ranks creators by local reach, not follower count, and Momentum adds the delivery record on top of it. If you have not read it yet, start with what local reach is and why brands care more than follower count.

How you earn it

What counts as posting on time?

The standard is stated before you claim anything, so there is no guessing later. For a comped visit, posting on time means 1 post or reel plus 1 story that shares the post, with the goal of posting within 48 hours of the visit, honest and disclosed, with the meal, service, or experience as the compensation. For a paid booking, it means delivering what that booking's terms say, on the schedule those terms set.

That is the whole deal. No extra deliverables appear after the visit. No business can require a positive post. Your honest take, posted on time and disclosed properly, is the product. For the full breakdown of the visit itself, read what a comped creator visit actually includes and FTC disclosure for comped posts.

/ 01

Claim what you can do

Only claim a visit when you can actually go and post inside the window. A visit you skip helps nobody, and it leaves a gap in the record businesses see.

/ 02

Post while it is fresh

Draft the caption at the table or in the chair, not three days later. Fresh detail reads better, performs better, and lands inside the 48-hour goal without a scramble.

/ 03

Let the record stack

Every on-time post adds to the same visible record. A string of reliable visits is the strongest pitch a small creator can make, because it is proof, not a promise.

Why businesses care

Why does a deadline decide your next booking?

A local business plans around your post. It staffs for the visit, it tells its regulars something is coming, and the post does its work while the visit is still recent enough for followers to act on it. A post that lands a week late is not the same product, even if the photo is great.

So owners do what anyone would do. They book the creator who delivered last time, and they hesitate over the creator whose record is blank or patchy. Momentum turns that private judgment into a fair, visible signal. A new creator with a small following can build it fast, which is part of how small accounts win work here. From comped to paid

How does Momentum turn into paid work?

Comped visits and paid bookings are two lanes, stated in the first breath. Take a comped visit, a meal, service, or experience in exchange for the standard post and story. Or be booked at your own listed rate for a paid booking, where you keep 97 percent and paid bookings start at a $50 floor.

Momentum connects the lanes. A clean run of on-time comped posts is the evidence a business uses when it decides to pay your rate instead of offering another comp. It does not change your rate and it does not discount it. It makes your rate easier to say yes to. The longer playbook is in how to turn a comped visit into paid work.

What else do creators ask about Momentum?

What are Momentum points on Magpie?

How do you earn Momentum points?

How does posting on time get you booked more?

Businesses book creators they trust to show up in their customers' feeds when the visit is still fresh. A record of on-time posts makes that trust visible before a business has met you. On Magpie, Momentum raises your visibility, so reliable creators are easier to find and easier to choose for the next offer or paid booking.

What happens if you post late or miss a visit?

A late post earns less than an on-time post, and a visit with no post does not build Momentum. Businesses plan around the post, so missing the window weakens the record a business sees next time. The fix is simple: only claim visits you can actually do, and post while the visit is still fresh.

Does Momentum replace local reach or follower count?

No. Magpie ranks and surfaces creators by local reach, not by follower count. Momentum is a second signal on top of that: local reach shows how much of your audience lives in the market, and Momentum shows how reliably you post. Paid bookings still work at a rate you set yourself, you keep 97 percent, and paid bookings start at a $50 floor.
