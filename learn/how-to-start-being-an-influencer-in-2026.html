How to start being an influencer in 2026: the step-by-step guide

By Jeff, Founder, SIGNL · October 22, 2026

You do not need a ring light empire, a manager or a mysterious brand deal to begin. You need a corner of the internet, a city you actually know, ten honest posts and one business willing to let you visit. Here is the road from zero to booked, in order.

What does starting actually mean?

Starting means making a public promise about what you post and then keeping it long enough for people and businesses to understand it. It does not mean quitting your job, buying gear or waiting until your feed looks like a magazine. Your first version can be simple, local and unmistakably yours.

SIGNL is free for creators. Always. Businesses pay the platform. You never pay to join, to list, or to get booked. Anyone can apply, and tiers are automatic: Neighbors at 500 to 2,000 total followers, Locals at 2,000 to 15,000, and Headliners at 15,000 or more. This guide assumes you are starting small and want the shortest honest route to a first booking.

Steps 1 to 3

Start with a corner, not a costume

Step 1

Pick your corner

Choose the subject you can post about when nobody is paying you. Food, coffee, family outings, salons, fitness, vintage shopping, campus life or date nights all work if they fit your real life. A narrow corner is easier to remember and easier for the right business to choose.

Step 2

Name your city and audience

Put your city in your bio and say who the page is for. “Trying every taco in Norfolk” is clearer than “lifestyle content.” Local businesses need to know your followers can visit, and local followers need to know your posts will help them decide where to go.

Step 3

Set up clean profiles

Use the same handle where you can, a recognizable photo and a bio with your city, niche and a way to reach you. Connect the platforms you genuinely use. Do not launch five empty accounts. One active home base plus one second platform is a perfectly respectable empire.

Steps 4 to 5

Make the first ten posts

Step 4

Plan ten posts before you pitch

Visit places you already like and can talk about honestly. Mix a review, a guide, a shortlist, a what-to-order post and a neighborhood walk. Ten posts give a business enough evidence to judge your voice, your camera habits and whether local people respond.

Step 5

Learn the visit rules early

A SIGNL Standard Visit is 1 post or reel plus 1 story that shares the post, posted within 48 hours and disclosed. Your opinion stays yours. A comped visit includes your post and the business sharing it natively. Paid visits add repost and ad use for 6 months from the post date, while you keep ownership of your content.

Disclosure is not an advanced topic for later. A free meal, service or experience is a material connection, so read FTC disclosure for comped posts before your first visit. Doing this properly is a green flag, not a buzzkill.

Steps 6 to 8

Get booked, then get paid

Step 6

Claim and complete your profile

Claim your SIGNL spot and finish the profile like a business will read it, because one will. Add your city and neighborhood, connect your platforms, show your best local work and state your categories. A half-finished profile asks a stranger to imagine your potential. A complete one shows the receipts.

Step 7

Land the first comped visit

Accept an offer or pitch one specific visit to one specific business. Lead with your local audience, propose the deliverables and keep the ask easy to answer. Our guide to how to pitch local businesses as a small creator gives you the structure and the promises to avoid.

Step 8

Post, report and set a rate

Visit like a good guest, post within 48 hours and send a short note with the result. Then list a paid rate for the next booking. Published SIGNL guidance is $25 to $75 for Neighbors, $75 to $250 for Locals and $250 to $750 for Headliners, with listed paid rates starting at $50. You keep 97 percent of a paid rate.

Your first paid rate is not a lifetime verdict. It is a starting line based on your tier, local audience and proof. For the full pricing logic, read how much a small creator should charge. If your audience is around 500, continue with how to be a creator with 500 followers for the Neighbors playbook.

The first month

What should the first month look like?

  • ✓Week one: choose the corner, clean up the profiles and draft the first ten post ideas.
  • ✓Week two: publish the first local posts and start replying like a future community leader, not a lighthouse.
  • ✓Week three: complete your SIGNL profile, study the visit rules and identify the businesses that fit your corner.
  • ✓Week four: pitch or apply for the first comped visit, deliver it well and write down what you learned.

That is not a promise that a booking lands in exactly four weeks. It is a workable rhythm that creates the things bookings require: evidence, visibility, a complete profile and a reputation for finishing. If you are deciding what kind of creator to become, compare yourself with the types of local creators businesses book first and pick the lane that sounds like your actual life.

FAQ

Questions, answered straight

How do I start being an influencer with no following?

Pick one local niche, put your city in your bio, publish ten useful posts about places you already know, and complete a creator profile. Then pitch or accept a specific comped visit with clear deliverables. You do not need a large following to start building the proof businesses use to book you.

What should my first ten posts be?

Use the places and routines already in your life: an honest review, a neighborhood guide, a shortlist, a what-to-order post, a first visit and a follow-up based on comments. The goal is not perfection. The goal is enough consistent local content for followers and businesses to understand your corner.

When should I join SIGNL?

Join when you are ready to be considered for local visits, even if your audience is still small. Anyone can apply, tiers are automatic, and a complete profile is what businesses review. SIGNL is free for creators, and paid bookings are priced at your own listed rate.

What is a Standard Visit?

A Standard Visit is 1 post or reel plus 1 story that shares the post, posted honestly within 48 hours of the visit and disclosed. The post stays live for at least 30 days under the published visit terms. It is the shared deliverable for comped visits and paid bookings.

How do I set my first paid rate?

Use your tier as a starting range: Neighbors $25 to $75, Locals $75 to $250 and Headliners $250 to $750 per post, with a $50 floor for listed SIGNL rates. Adjust for local audience, engagement, niche fit and a record of posting on time. You keep 97 percent of a paid rate.
