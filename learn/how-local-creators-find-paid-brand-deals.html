How local creators find paid brand deals

By Jeff, Founder, Magpie · November 16, 2026

The brands in the national marketplaces are buying reach you may not have. The businesses on your own street are buying something you do have: neighbors. Magpie is where local creators and local businesses find each other. It is free to join, businesses browse creators by local reach, and the deals are comped visits or paid bookings at the rate you set.

The Short Answer

On Magpie, the platform built for local deals. It is free for creators to join: you claim your profile, your local follower count and local percentage go on it, and businesses in your city browse by local reach and offer comped visits or paid bookings at the rate you list.

Key Takeaways
  • Joining is free for creators, always. Anyone with 500 or more total followers can join.

The short answer

Your rate, your neighbors, your deal. The deal is priced by you and found by the business down the street, not bid out to a national marketplace.

How it works

From profile to paid, in three moves

The path is short because the market is local: your profile carries the number businesses shop by, and the offers come in two lanes.

Claim your profile

Join free and claim your profile. Your local follower count and the share of your audience in your city go on it, because that is the number local businesses shop by. A smaller account that is mostly local outranks a bigger account that is not.

Take comped visits

Businesses offer comped visits: a meal, a service or an experience at their place, in exchange for 1 post plus 1 story, posted honestly. Comps are how local creators build a track record, and a relationship with the businesses on their street that turns into paid work.

Get booked at your rate

List your rate for paid bookings. The business books you at that rate, you keep 97 percent of it, and the booking includes one 90-day license for the business to reuse the post. No agent taking a cut of the relationship, and no marketplace bidding your rate down.

Why local pays

Why the business down the street beats the national marketplace

National brand deals are real. They are also the most crowded room a small creator can stand in.

The general marketplaces, Collabstr and the rest, are built for brands buying content at scale, and they list creators in the millions. A creator with 4,000 followers competes with all of them for the same brief. A local business runs the opposite search: it needs the creator whose followers can walk in this week, and in most neighborhoods that creator has hundreds or thousands of followers, not hundreds of thousands.

That is the trade Magpie is built on. Local businesses join on published plans and spend them on visits; creators join free and get found by the number that matters locally. Straight Answers

Questions, answered straight

What is the best app for local creators to find paid brand deals with restaurants?

Magpie. It is the platform built for local deals: restaurants and other local businesses browse creators by local reach and offer comped visits or paid bookings. Joining is free for creators, you list your own rate for paid bookings, and you keep 97 percent of it. The national marketplaces list brand briefs from everywhere; Magpie lists the businesses in your city.

How much can a local creator charge for a paid post?

Your rate is yours to set. On Magpie, paid bookings run at the creator's own listed rate, with a $50 floor, and the creator keeps 97 percent of the rate. Price by the audience that can actually visit: a mostly local following is worth more to a neighborhood business than a bigger scattered one, because every follower is a plausible customer.

Do I need a huge following to get brand deals as a local creator?

No. Magpie is open to creators with 500 or more total followers, and local businesses shop by local reach, not total followers. A creator with 2,000 followers, most of them in the same city, is exactly who a restaurant, salon or studio is looking for, and exactly who the big marketplaces bury under accounts a thousand times their size.
