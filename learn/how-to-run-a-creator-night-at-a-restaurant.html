How to run a creator night at a restaurant

A creator night is not a party with phones out. It is a working night: a small group of local creators, hosted on purpose, on one clear deliverable each, in a room that still runs like a normal service. Get the group, the deliverable, and the staff brief right, and the posts take care of themselves.

By Jeff, Founder, Magpie · October 31, 2026 · Magpie Learn hub

The short answer

Why run creators on one night instead of one at a time?

One creator on a random Tuesday is a comp. Four to six creators on one planned night is a moment: the room feels alive, the kitchen cooks the menu you actually want talked about, and a cluster of honest local posts lands inside the same 48 hour window. People nearby see the same place from several trusted accounts in two days, not one post they scroll past in March and another in June.

The night only works if it stays a real visit. Creators sit, order, and experience the restaurant the way a paying guest does. The moment staff start staging a performance for the cameras, the posts turn into ads, and ads are not what anyone booked. The standard behind every visit is in what a comped creator visit actually includes.

The group

Who should be on the invite list?

Pick for local reach, not follower count. A creator with a smaller account and an audience that lives nearby will put more potential guests in your seats than a larger account whose followers are everywhere else. That is the whole point of what local reach is, and a creator night is where it pays off fastest, because every post points at the same room on the same weekend.

  • ✓Keep it small: 4 to 6 creators for a first night. Enough for a cluster of posts, small enough for the kitchen and floor to run clean.
  • ✓Mix the angles, not the audience: a food account, a local life account, and a date night account can all cover the same menu differently. What they should share is an audience that lives near you.
  • ✓State the guest count: say exactly how many people each comp covers, so nobody guesses at the table and nobody is embarrassed at the bill.
  • ✓Pick a night with a job: a new menu, a slow weekday you want to build, or a room that deserves a fuller house. A creator night with no job becomes a free dinner with extra steps.
The deliverable

What does each creator owe you, exactly?

One clear deliverable, written down before anyone sits down. On a comped visit on Magpie, that deliverable is fixed: 1 post or reel plus 1 story that shares the post, with the goal of posting within 48 hours of the visit, honest and disclosed. On a paid booking, the deliverables are set in the booking terms, at the creator's own rate, with a $50 floor, and the creator keeps 97 percent.

Brief facts, not lines. Share the must-mentions, the timing, the comp ceiling, and the disclosure requirement, then let each creator cover the night in their own voice. The full method is in how to brief a creator. Five creators reading the same script produce one weak post five times. Five creators having a real night produce five different reasons to visit.

Keep the lanes separate on the night itself. A creator is either on a comped visit or on a paid booking for that visit, never a blend of both, and the terms for their lane are the terms that govern their post.

Run the night

How do you run it in three moves?

/ 01

Brief staff before doors

Five minutes, before service: who is hosting, which creators are expected, what each comp covers and its ceiling, and who a creator asks for on arrival. Most creator nights that go wrong go wrong here, with a floor team hearing about it from the first creator at the host stand.

/ 02

Seat them like guests

Creators redeem in the app on the single use code tied to the visit, then they are guests. Normal pacing, normal menu, no hovering and no staged moments. A post about a real night is the asset. A post about being managed is not.

/ 03

Close the loop fast

On a comped visit, the goal is for the post to land within 48 hours of the visit. Watch for them, reshare natively with credit while they are fresh, and log who posted on time. On Magpie, posting on time earns Momentum points and raises a creator's visibility, which is why reliable creators take the deadline seriously.

If a creator does not post, do not turn it into a chase at the host stand next week. A tracked visit with no post does not count and is replaced. That remedy has to exist before the night, not be improvised after it.

After the night

What turns one night into a habit?

Judge the night on what you can count: posts delivered on time, and what came back at the register. The counting method is in how to track influencer marketing ROI. Then make the follow up move that compounds: invite the strongest fits back. A creator who has covered your room twice reads as a regular, and regulars are who new guests believe.

Handle reuse honestly. A comped post is native share only on every plan: you may reshare it natively with credit, and that is the limit. To run a comped post as an ad, send a license request at the creator's listed licensing fee, for 90 days, and the creator approves each use and keeps 97 percent. A paid booking already includes one standard license: 90 days of repost and paid ad use, inside the booking terms. The distinction between buying a post and buying reusable content is covered in UGC vs. influencer posts for local businesses.

Cost the night inside a plan, not as a one off splurge. A 5 visit month can be one creator night. A 10 visit month can be two, with change.

Business FAQ

What else do restaurants ask about creator nights?

What is a creator night at a restaurant?

A creator night is a small, planned evening where a restaurant hosts a handful of local creators on the same night, each on a clear visit with a clear deliverable, instead of scattered one off comps across a month. Done right, the posts land in a tight window, the room has real energy, and each creator reaches a local audience that can actually visit.

How many creators should a restaurant invite to a creator night?

Start with 4 to 6 creators for a first night. That is enough to fill a section and create a cluster of posts, and small enough that the kitchen, the bar, and the floor team can still give every guest a real experience. Scale up only after the first night runs clean.

What do creators post after a creator night?

On a comped visit on Magpie, each creator posts 1 post or reel plus 1 story that shares the post, with the goal of posting within 48 hours of the visit, honest and disclosed. On a paid booking, the deliverables are the ones set in the booking terms, at the creator's own rate, with a $50 floor, and the creator keeps 97 percent.

How should staff handle creator night visits?

Brief staff before doors open: who is hosting, what each comp covers and its ceiling, and who a creator should ask for on arrival. On the night, treat creators like normal guests and handle redemption in the Magpie app on the single use code tied to the visit. No special performance, no voucher handling at the register, and no surprises for the floor team.

What should a restaurant do after creator night?

Follow up while the posts are fresh. Thank each creator, reshare their posts natively with credit, and note who posted on time and whose audience responded. A comped post is native share only, so if you want to run one as an ad, send a license request at the creator's listed licensing fee for 90 days. Invite the strongest fits back as regulars instead of starting over with strangers next month.
