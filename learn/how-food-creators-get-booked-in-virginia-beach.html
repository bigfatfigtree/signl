Comped and paid

What is the difference between a comped meal and a paid booking?

A comped visit means the meal itself is the pay. You deliver one post or reel plus one story that shares the post, honest and disclosed, with a goal of posting within 48 hours. Keep the post live for 30 or more days. It is a fair, clear trade for a first visit with a restaurant.

A paid booking is cash work at your own listed rate. You keep 97 percent, and the booking includes one standard license for 90 days so the business can reuse your content for a season. Set a rate that fits the work, update it as your reach and Momentum grow, and keep it honest.

Many creators start comped, prove they deliver, then move to paid bookings with the same restaurants. A seafood place on Shore Drive that trusts your timing and your honesty will call you first for a new menu, a paid launch post, and the slow winter weeks when it needs locals most.

Delivering well

How do food creators get booked again in Virginia Beach?

Businesses rebook creators who make the visit easy. Arrive on time, confirm the dishes in advance, film without blocking service, and post inside the 48 hour goal. Reply to local comments asking for the address or the dish name. Those small acts are remembered longer than a clever caption or a trending sound.

On Magpie, Momentum is your reliability record, built by delivering on time. Strong Momentum tells a busy owner in Red Mill or Hilltop that you will do what you promised during their busiest week. It protects you, too, because your record travels with your profile to every new business.

Use the checklist in this section for every visit. Taste everything you feature, disclose the comp clearly, and say what you really thought in your own words. Honest posts, including a fair note about what you would order differently, build the trust that keeps both followers and restaurants coming back.

  • ✓Confirm the dishes.: Agree what you will taste and feature before you arrive, and note any allergies or items you will not post.

  • ✓Answer local comments.: Reply with the neighborhood, the dish name, and practical tips so followers can act on the post fast.
Posting like a local

What should Virginia Beach food creators post to attract bookings?
