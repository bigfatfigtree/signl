Dallas–Fort Worth creators to watch

Before Magpie launches in Dallas–Fort Worth, our editors went looking for the creators a local business should know first. Not the biggest accounts with a Dallas pin in the bio. The ones who live here and post for their neighbors, and show up week after week. Forty made the list.

What “to watch” means

Rooted here, watched here, posting like it matters

Three tests put a creator on this list. First, rooted here: they live here, from Dallas and Fort Worth out to Frisco, Plano, Arlington, and Southlake, and their beat is our own restaurants, rooms, teams, and weekends. Second, an audience that is actually local, as far as public evidence shows: the comments, the places, the coverage all point home. That idea has a name, local reach, and it is the number Magpie puts in front of businesses. Third, consistent craft: posting recently and regularly, not one viral weekend a year ago.

This is an editorial list, picked by hand from public profiles, and it is honest about what it is not. It is not a follower-count ranking. The accounts here range from 824 followers to 395,000, and every one earned its place the same way. It is also not a score table: these are editors’ picks, grouped by what they do, not ranked by size, and the full list lives on the Dallas–Fort Worth Creators to Watch page.
