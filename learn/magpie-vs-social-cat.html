Magpie vs Social Cat: a local roster or a gifting matchmaker?

Social Cat and Magpie both aim at the same customer, the small business that cannot hire an agency, and they get there differently. Social Cat matches small brands with vetted micro influencers for gifted and paid campaigns, mostly on Instagram and TikTok. Magpie gives a local business its own city's roster, ranked by how much of each creator's audience lives in that city, and the booking is a visit in person. This page compares the two honestly.

By Jeff, Founder, Magpie · November 22, 2026 · Magpie Learn hub

The Short Answer

Social Cat is a matchmaking platform for small brands: create a campaign, and vetted micro influencers apply, or search its database and invite them, with gifted, paid, and affiliate collaborations and content usage rights included, for $99 to $299 a month with no contract. Magpie is a local influencer platform: a business browses its own city's roster, ranked by local reach, and books in-person visits, comped or paid, at published prices. Product brands that ship gifts to creators pick Social Cat; local businesses that host creators in person pick Magpie.

Key Takeaways
  • Social Cat runs on matching: brands post campaigns, manually vetted creators apply, and plans cap collaborations per month (5, 15, or 30) with usage rights included on every plan.
  • Social Cat covers Instagram and TikTok, in the United States, United Kingdom, Australia, and New Zealand, and its gifted model is built around shipping product to creators.
  • Magpie's comped visit is the in-person version of a gifted collab: the creator comes to the business, and the roster is ranked by how much of the creator's audience lives in your city.
Their model

What Social Cat does well

Social Cat is built for a small brand's first fifty creator posts. A brand creates a campaign in minutes, vetted micro influencers apply to it, and the brand can also search a database it puts at 500,000 influencers and invite creators directly. Every influencer is vetted manually before approval, collaborations can be gifted, paid, or affiliate, and content usage rights come included, which many platforms sell separately. Pricing is transparent and month to month: $99, $199, or $299 depending on how many collaborations a month the brand runs, cancel anytime.

For an online store, that is a clean deal. The product ships, the posts go up, and the brand owns the content for its ads and product pages.

Our model

Where Magpie is different

Magpie is for the business that cannot ship its product, because the product is the place: the meal, the haircut, the workout, the fitting room. Its comped visit works like a gifted collaboration, except the creator comes to the business instead of receiving a package, tries the real thing in front of their followers, and posts a post and a story. Paid bookings work at the creator's own listed rate, and creators keep 97 percent of what they earn.

The roster is also organized differently. Social Cat matches by niche and campaign fit across four countries. Magpie organizes by city and ranks by local reach: the share of a creator's audience that lives in your city and the local follower count are on every profile before you choose. A creator can be a perfect niche match and still be wrong for a neighborhood business if their followers live somewhere else. Magpie is in its founding phase, with rosters forming in 53 markets and markets opening city by city, and we say so plainly. The category is defined on the local influencer platform page.

The decision

Which one fits you?

Pick Social Cat if you sell a product online, you want vetted micro influencers applying to your campaigns, and gifted posts with usage rights are the model you want. Pick Magpie if you run a physical local business, the experience has to happen at your place, and you want a city roster ranked by how local each creator's audience is.

One ships product to creators; the other brings creators through your door. More comparisons live in the Learn hub, including Magpie vs Collabstr and Magpie vs Heepsy.

Straight Answers

Magpie vs Social Cat, in plain answers

What is the difference between Magpie and Social Cat?

Social Cat matches small brands with vetted micro influencers for gifted, paid, and affiliate campaigns on Instagram and TikTok, mostly for products that ship. Magpie is a local platform where a business books creators from its own city, ranked by how many of their followers live nearby, for in-person visits, comped or paid, at published prices.

How much does Social Cat cost?

Social Cat charges $99, $199, or $299 a month depending on the plan, with collaboration caps of 5, 15, or 30 a month, no contract, and cancel anytime. Magpie charges businesses a published founding subscription of $199, $349, or $699 a month with a 3-month minimum, and creators join Magpie free.

What is the difference between a gifted collaboration and a comped visit?

A gifted collaboration sends free product to a creator, who posts about it from wherever they are. A comped visit brings the creator into the business: they experience the meal, service, or class in person and post about the visit. Magpie is built around the visit, because a local business sells an experience at a place, not a product in a box.

Which is better for a local restaurant or salon?

For a single-location business, Magpie is built for that: a city roster ranked by local reach, and visits that happen at your business. Social Cat is built for product brands that ship gifts to creators across the US, UK, Australia, and New Zealand.
