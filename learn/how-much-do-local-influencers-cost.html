How much do local influencers cost?

By Jeff, Founder, SIGNL · October 21, 2026

Three numbers answer it. A comped local visit costs $0 in cash: the creator is paid in your product, at your product cost, not menu price. A paid local post costs $25-750 depending on the creator's tier. A SIGNL plan costs $299, $599 or $999 a month depending on how many creator visits you want. Everything below is how those numbers compare, and what moves them.

Option one · Comps

What does a comped visit actually cost you?

For most local businesses, the cheapest local influencer is the one who never invoices you. In a comped visit, the business provides a meal or service, the creator visits like a real customer, and the creator posts honestly within 48 hours, FTC disclosed. The full mechanics are in our guide to what a comped creator visit is.

The cost is real, but it is not the sticker price. A dinner with a $100 menu value costs a restaurant its food cost for that dinner, not $100, because the business pays in its own product. A salon fills a chair that would sit empty on a Tuesday. That is why comped visits are the default rail on SIGNL: marginal cost is a fraction of the value the creator receives, and no cash changes hands.

Two rules keep the math honest. The business sets the comp and its ceiling on every offer, so the cost is chosen, never open-ended. And the comp is separate from the plan: SIGNL never touches comp money and adds no per-creator cash fee on standard visits.

Option two · Paid posts

What does a paid local post cost, by tier?

When a business wants a specific creator, a launch date, or a bigger name, it can book a paid post instead of a comp. On SIGNL the creator sets their own rate. These are the guidance bands by tier:

Neighbors
500 - 2k followers
  • Paid post guidance: $25-75 per post
  • Everyday locals whose followers are their actual neighbors
  • Most Neighbors work for comps; paid bookings are the exception
Locals
2k - 15k followers
  • Paid post guidance: $75-250 per post
  • Regular local posters, the roster's sweet spot
  • The tier most paid local bookings land in
Headliners
15k+ followers
  • Paid post guidance: $250-750 per post
  • Established local creators with the biggest local audiences
  • First pick for grand openings and launches

The platform fee on a paid booking is 6 percent total, split evenly, so the creator keeps 97 percent of their rate, and payment is held by SIGNL until the post is live. For market context: Collabstr reports from 21,000+ collaborations that 80 percent of engagements cost under $300 and the average UGC collaboration runs about $180. Local paid posts sit in that range, with one difference: the audience is in your city.

Option three · Platforms

What do platforms and agencies charge instead?

The platform fee is where budgets quietly blow up. Here is what the alternatives charge, from SIGNL's October 2026 competitor research.

Enterprise

Aspire: about $2,299/mo

Reported pricing for Aspire's Essentials tier runs about $2,299 a month on a 12-month contract, plus a one-time $2,000 onboarding fee: roughly 8 times SIGNL Self-Service, before a single creator is paid.

Managed

Insense managed: from $1,600/mo

Insense publishes a Dedicated Platform Manager from $1,600 a month, on top of its self-serve platform ($500 a month billed quarterly, or $400 billed annually) and a 7-20 percent fee on creator payments. Human help is the expensive layer, everywhere.

Marketplace

#paid: $699/mo for Grow

#paid's reported tiers run from $499 a month for Just Content to $699 for Grow and $999 for Advanced, creator payments extra. A curated marketplace for national brands, not a local visit platform.

The local-first comparisons are closer to home. Mustard, restaurants only and fully automated, publishes $299 a month with 3 influencer visits included and no human support on the base plan. Storytime, the local model in New York, New Jersey and Miami, was reported by Inc. and QSR Magazine at from $150 a month for 15 creators a month. Momfluence runs managed campaigns at $2,000 per campaign for 10 creators, product supplied on top.

Against that field, SIGNL pricing is published and flat: $299 a month for 8 visits (about $37 a visit), $599 for 20 visits (about $30 a visit), or $999 for 30 visits plus a coordinator, 3-month minimum, then month to month. A human running your visits costs $999 a month here, against $1,600 a month and up above.

Price drivers

What actually drives a local influencer's price?

Four things move the number. Knowing them keeps you from overpaying for reach you cannot use.

/ 01

Tier and local share

Follower tier sets the band, but local audience percentage sets the value. A creator with 8,000 followers, 70 percent in your market, delivers more potential customers than one with 80,000 spread across the country. Price the local number, not the headline.

/ 02

Deliverables

One post costs less than a post plus stories plus a second platform. The SIGNL Standard Visit fixes the package up front: 1 post or reel plus 2 stories, live for 30 or more days, tracked. Anything beyond it is where prices climb.

/ 03

Usage rights and exclusivity

Running a creator's post as your own ad, or barring a creator from competitors, is a separate, priced thing on every serious platform. If you do not need ad rights or exclusivity, do not pay for them.

The monthly math is simple: plan price, plus comps at your cost, plus any paid posts you choose. A restaurant on Growth running 20 comped visits pays $599 plus the food cost of 20 meals it chose and capped itself. The same 20 posts bought one by one at the Collabstr average of about $180 would run about $3,600 in creator fees alone. Weighing creator size rather than price? FAQ

Questions, answered straight

How much does a local influencer cost per post?

Paid local posts run $25-750 on SIGNL rate guidance: $25-75 for Neighbors (500-2,000 followers), $75-250 for Locals (2,000-15,000) and $250-750 for Headliners (15,000+). Creators set their own rates, so treat the bands as guidance, not a price list. A comped visit costs no cash at all: the creator is paid in your product, at your cost.

Is a comped visit really free?

It is free of cash fees to the creator, not free of cost to you. You provide the meal or service, and it costs you your product cost, not the menu price. On SIGNL the comp is separate from your plan, you set the comp and its ceiling on every offer, and SIGNL never touches comp money.

What does SIGNL charge a local business?

Three published plans: Self-Service is $299 a month for up to 8 creator visits a month, Growth is $599 a month for up to 20 visits, and Managed is $999 a month for up to 30 visits plus a coordinator. Plans carry a 3-month minimum, then run month to month. The comp you offer creators is separate and paid by you in product.

Why do agencies and national platforms cost so much more?

They are built for national brands with marketing teams. Reported pricing includes Aspire at about $2,299 a month plus a $2,000 onboarding fee on 12-month terms, and Insense managed service from $1,600 a month. Those tools sell national reach, CRM features and content licensing. A local business pays for all of it whether it uses it or not.

Do local creators set their own rates?

On SIGNL, yes. A creator sets their own paid rate inside the guidance bands for their tier. The platform fee is 6 percent total, split evenly between creator and business, so the creator keeps 97 percent of their rate. Payment is held by SIGNL until the post is live.
