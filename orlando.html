For creators: the deal, up front

SIGNL is free for creators. Always. Businesses pay the platform. You never pay to join, to list, or to get booked.

Comp visits are the core. Paid bookings sit on top: set your own rate, the business pays it, and the platform fee is 6 percent total, split evenly, so you keep 97 percent of your rate. Paid bookings are held by SIGNL until your post is live. Headliners (15,000+ followers) are also first in line for paid hero campaigns when businesses run them.

Why Orlando should open

The locals' Orlando is the story

A food city hiding in plain sight

Mills 50 and the Milk District run on independent kitchens, bakeries and bars that most visitors never hear about. Locals eat extremely well here, and they post about it to each other, not to tourists.

UCF is a small city

One of the largest universities in America sits on Orlando's east side, tens of thousands of students deciding where to eat, drink and get their hair cut within a few miles of campus.

Neighborhood pride, block by block

Winter Park polish, Thornton Park porches, College Park main street: Orlando's districts have loyal regulars and strong opinions, the raw material of a creator roster.

Hospitality workers live here

The people who run the visitor economy live in these neighborhoods and go out in them. Nobody knows Orlando's food scene better than the people who staff it.

Creator tiers

Where do you land?

Tiers are set automatically by your total audience across platforms, and they rise as you grow. A small, genuinely local audience beats a big, scattered one.

Neighbors
500 – 2k followers
  • Everyday people with a local following
  • Comp only
  • Perfect first step into local posting
Locals
2k – 15k followers
  • Regular local posters, the roster's sweet spot
  • Comp plus roster perks
  • Priority access to new businesses
Headliners
15k+ followers
  • Established local creators
  • Comp plus eligibility for paid hero campaigns
  • First pick of grand openings and launches

Under 500 followers, or just starting out? Apply anyway. Your tier is set by your follower count, and it moves up as you grow. What matters most is not the number, it's that your audience is actually local and actually engages. Pick your band honestly when you sign up.

Orlando FAQ

Questions, answered straight

Is SIGNL open in Orlando yet?

No, and we will not pretend otherwise. Orlando is not one of SIGNL's 39 founding markets yet, so there are no offers and no roster there today. Sign-ups from Orlando are counted city by city, and the cities with the most sign-ups open first. Sign up now and you are first on the list when Orlando opens.

How do I sign up if Orlando is not listed?

In the SIGNL app, choose My city is not listed yet and type Orlando. That is a real sign-up, counted toward opening Orlando, and it puts you at the front of its founding list if the city opens. It takes about two minutes.

Does it cost anything to join SIGNL as a creator?

No. SIGNL is free for creators. Always. Businesses pay the platform. You never pay to join, to list, or to get booked. Your compensation on standard visits is the comp itself: the meal, service or experience the business provides.

What would I actually post about in Orlando?

A Mills 50 pho verdict, a Milk District night, Park Avenue in Winter Park, a Thornton Park wine walk. The Standard Visit is 1 post or reel plus 2 stories, live within 48 hours of the visit, honest and clearly disclosed as comped.

I am a student at UCF. Can I join?

Yes. Name your school when you sign up or finish your profile and your profile carries your campus, so businesses near your school can find you first. The Standard Visit fits between classes, and alcohol offers are 21+ only.
