Christchurch FAQ

Straight answers

Is Magpie live in Christchurch yet?

Founding sign-ups are open in Christchurch now. Offers launch in waves as the founding roster fills. Creators who sign up now are founding creators: their status and their place in application order never expire. Businesses join the founding list now and activate when the market opens.

How do I join Magpie as a creator in Christchurch?

Sign up free in the Magpie app at app.magpielocal.com with Christchurch as your market and your real home city. Magpie starts at 500 total followers across your platforms. Your tier is set by followers: Neighbors (500 to 2,000), Locals (2,000 to 15,000) or Headliners (15,000+). When Christchurch opens, founding creators see every comp offer first, from Riverside Market stalls to Merivale dining rooms.

How does a Christchurch business join?

Apply for the founding business list on the business page. Your category spot in your trade area is held in application order, founding pricing (NZ$349 a month Self-Service, NZ$619 a month Growth or NZ$1,239 a month Managed, all plus GST) is held while you remain subscribed, and your plan begins when the Christchurch market launches. Nothing is charged today.

What does Magpie cost a business in Christchurch?

Published pricing for Christchurch: NZ$349 a month for Self-Service (up to 5 creator visits a month, about NZ$70 per creator visit), NZ$619 a month for Growth (up to 10 creator visits a month, about NZ$62 per creator visit) or NZ$1,239 a month for Managed (up to 20 creator visits a month), with a 3-month minimum and then month to month. Founding businesses get NZ$175 off the first month, and all prices are plus GST. Visits you don't use roll into the next month, then expire. The only other cost is the comp the business chooses to offer, provided at its cost, not retail.
