Virginia Beach FAQ

Straight answers

Is Magpie live in Virginia Beach yet?

Founding sign-ups are open in Virginia Beach now, and the market covers all of Hampton Roads. Offers launch in waves as the founding roster fills. Creators who sign up now are founding creators: their status and their place in application order never expire. Businesses join the founding list now and activate when the market opens.

What is Magpie?

Magpie is the local micro-influencer platform for Hampton Roads, anchored in Virginia Beach. Local businesses book local creators, ranked by local reach (the local share of a creator's audience) rather than follower count. Businesses offer a comped visit (a meal, a service or an experience) or a paid booking at the creator's own listed rate. Magpie is built and rolling out market by market, and the founding roster here is forming now.

How do Hampton Roads businesses find micro-influencers on Magpie?

They browse the founding roster, where every creator is ranked by local audience share first and follower count second, and send an offer: a comped visit, a paid booking at the creator's listed rate, or a coupon deal. Every visit is booked, tracked and counted in the platform, and creators are found by the local audience they actually have. No agency sits in the middle, and no creator rate is ever marked up.

How do I join Magpie as a creator in Virginia Beach?

Sign up free in the Magpie app at app.magpielocal.com with Virginia Beach as your market (it covers all of Hampton Roads) and your real home city. Magpie starts at 500 total followers across your platforms. Your tier is set by followers: Neighbors (500 to 2,000), Locals (2,000 to 15,000) or Headliners (15,000+). When the Virginia Beach market opens, founding creators see every comp offer first.

How does a Virginia Beach business join?

Apply for the founding business list on the business page. Your category spot in your trade area is held in application order, founding pricing ($199 a month Self-Service, $349 a month Growth or $699 a month Managed) is held while you remain subscribed, and your plan begins when the Virginia Beach market launches. Nothing is charged today.

What does Magpie cost a business in Virginia Beach?

Published pricing, the same in every market: $199 a month for Self-Service (up to 5 creator visits a month, about $40 per creator visit), $349 a month for Growth (up to 10 creator visits a month, about $35 per creator visit) or $699 a month for Managed (up to 20 creator visits a month), with a 3-month minimum and then month to month. Founding businesses get $100 off the first month. Visits you don't use roll into the next month, then expire. The only other cost is the comp the business chooses to offer, provided at its cost, not retail.

How much do Hampton Roads creators earn on Magpie?

Joining is free, always. On comped visits your pay is the experience itself, and your rate stays published for paid work. Paid bookings start at a $50 floor, you set your own rate, and you keep 97% of every paid booking. If a business wants to reuse a comped post as an ad, that is a separate license at a fee you set: you approve it, it runs 90 days, and you keep 97% of the license fee too.

What happens on a Magpie visit in Hampton Roads?

You book a time, visit like a real customer, and the goal is to post 1 post plus 1 story within 48 hours of your visit, honest and clearly disclosed as comped. A post and a story are all a business can ask for on a comped visit. If a week passes with no post, the visit is replaced for the business, and creators who do not post lose standing on the roster.

What kinds of businesses use Magpie in Hampton Roads?

Restaurants and seafood houses first, then cafes, salons, barbershops, fitness studios, surf and skate shops, attractions and local services across all ten cities. If your customers physically visit you and your regulars talk about you, your category belongs on the founding roster.

Can a Hampton Roads business require a positive review?

No. No creator on Magpie can ever be required to post positively, on any plan or any deal. What a business is guaranteed is the deliverable: a real visit, and an honest post and story inside the standard. A business that pressures a creator for a positive post loses its account.

Why does Magpie rank Hampton Roads creators by local reach instead of followers?

Because a Hampton Roads business needs people who can walk in this week. Local reach is the share of a creator's audience that lives in Hampton Roads, and it is the headline number on every profile, ahead of follower count. In a beach market it does real work: it separates the year-round local whose followers live here from the travel account whose audience visited once in July.

My town is near Hampton Roads but not on the list. What should I do?

If your town is one of the 10 cities above, you are in the Virginia Beach market. If it is just outside the list, sign up in the app with your real home city anyway: unlisted sign-ups start your city's own list, your founding place is held in sign-up order, and cities with the most sign-ups are how Magpie picks what opens next.
