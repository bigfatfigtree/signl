Minneapolis FAQ

Straight answers

Is Magpie live in Minneapolis yet?

Founding sign-ups are open in Minneapolis now. Offers launch in waves as the founding roster fills. Creators who sign up now are founding creators: their status and their place in application order never expire. Businesses join the founding list now and activate when the market opens.

How do I join Magpie as a creator in Minneapolis?

Sign up free in the Magpie app at app.magpielocal.com with Minneapolis as your market and your real home city. Magpie starts at 500 total followers across your platforms. Your tier is set by followers: Neighbors (500 to 2,000), Locals (2,000 to 15,000) or Headliners (15,000+). When Minneapolis opens, founding creators see every comp offer first.

How does a Minneapolis business join?

Apply for the founding business list on the business page. Your category spot in your trade area is held in application order, founding pricing ($199 a month Self-Service, $349 a month Growth or $699 a month Managed) is held while you remain subscribed, and your plan begins when the Minneapolis market launches. Nothing is charged today.

What does Magpie cost a business in Minneapolis?

Published pricing, the same in every market: $199 a month for Self-Service (up to 5 creator visits a month, about $40 per creator visit), $349 a month for Growth (up to 10 creator visits a month, about $35 per creator visit) or $699 a month for Managed (up to 20 creator visits a month), with a 3-month minimum and then month to month. Founding businesses get $100 off the first month. Visits you don't use roll into the next month, then expire. The only other cost is the comp the business chooses to offer, provided at its cost, not retail.

I live near Minneapolis, not in it. Can I still join?

Yes. Sign up in the app with your real home city. Founding sign-ups are open in Minneapolis now, and creators in any city can apply once they reach 500 total followers across their platforms: if your city is not a listed market yet, your sign-up starts its list, your founding place is held in sign-up order, and signup counts are how Magpie picks what opens next.
