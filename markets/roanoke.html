Roanoke FAQ

Straight answers

Is SIGNL live in Roanoke yet?

Founding sign-ups are open in Roanoke now. Offers launch in waves as the founding roster fills, inside the 30 to 90 day launch window. Creators who sign up now are founding creators: their status and their place in application order never expire. Businesses join the founding list now and activate when the market opens.

How do I join SIGNL as a creator in Roanoke?

Sign up free in the SIGNL app at app.joinsignl.com with Roanoke as your market and your real home city. Anyone can apply at any follower count. Your tier is set by followers: Neighbors (500 to 2,000), Locals (2,000 to 15,000) or Creators (15,000+). When Roanoke opens, founding creators see every comp offer first.

How does a Roanoke business join?

Apply for the founding business list on the business page. Your category spot in your trade area is held in application order, founding pricing ($299 a month Self-Service, $599 a month Growth or $999 a month Managed) is locked while you remain subscribed, and your plan begins when the Roanoke market launches. Nothing is charged today.

What does SIGNL cost a business in Roanoke?

Published pricing, the same in every market: $299 a month for Self-Service (up to 8 creator visits a month, about $37 per creator visit), $599 a month for Growth (up to 20 creator visits a month, about $30 per creator visit) or $999 a month for Managed (up to 30 creator visits a month), with a 3-month minimum and then month to month. The only other cost is the comp the business chooses to offer, provided at its cost, not retail.

My town is near Roanoke but not on the list. What should I do?

If your town is one of the 9 cities above, you are in the Roanoke market. If it is just outside the list, sign up in the app with your real home city anyway: unlisted sign-ups start your city's own list, your founding place is held in sign-up order, and cities with the most sign-ups are how SIGNL picks what opens next. You can also put your city on the board with the form on this page.
