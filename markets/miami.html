The Magpie way

Names are for standards. Brands stay out.

Local creators post off their own feeds, which stay visible in the standard materials and the client templates. Local reach is what we live by: a creator’s local percentage is shown before any business chooses, ranked over follower count. Places get on the business side by business name only at the launch upsell. The verified-name standard covers creator names and the talent brand materials verbatim.

Local reach first

Ask what share of their audience actually lives here, and ask it before follower count. In Miami that comes with a second number, the share of the audience that speaks Spanish, and both are shown pre-choice.

A defined deliverable

One post plus one story that shares that post, with a goal of posting within 48 hours of the visit, bonded by the Visit Guarantee. A visit that never posts is replaced and does not count, which is what makes comped creator marketing accountable instead of hopeful.

Published pricing

$199, $349 or $699 a month with five, ten or twenty creator visits, founding credit of $100 off the first month, three-month minimum, no per-location charge, and a one-month rollover on unused visits. Everything is on the pricing page in the open.

How Magpie works in Miami

Comped first, paid on top

The standard: comped visits

Businesses invite creators in with a comped meal, service or experience. The creator visits like a real customer, then posts honestly, with the goal of posting within 48 hours of the visit: 1 post or reel plus 1 story that shares the post, with clear gifted disclosure. Every visit is tracked on one dashboard.

Nothing about the visit depends on the creator liking it. Reviews stay honest, which is why locals trust them.

On top: paid bookings

When a business wants more, it books the creator at their listed rate, from $50, on any plan. The creator keeps 97 percent of the rate; the platform fee is 6 percent total, split evenly between the two sides. Payment is held by Magpie until the post is live.

Licensing a comped post is a separate request at the creator’s listed licensing fee, for 90 days of repost and paid ad use, approved by the creator each time.

Miami FAQ

Questions, answered straight

Is Magpie live in Miami yet?

The market is pre-launch: offers begin when the market opens, and businesses sign up when we are ready to launch. Right now founding creators are being secured and businesses are joining the founding list, with spots held in application order. Creators can sign up today at no cost and will see every offer first when the market opens.

Can I join Magpie in Spanish?

Yes. Miami is Magpie’s Spanish-first founding market: the founding page has a full Spanish edition, sign-up and profile setup run in Spanish, and businesses can apply from the Spanish business page and be answered in Spanish. The rest of the app is English for now, and Spanish keeps growing.

What does it cost creators in Miami?

Nothing. Magpie is free for creators, always. Businesses pay for the platform; a creator never pays to join, to hold a roster spot, or to receive a booking.

What do Miami creators receive on a standard visit?

The business comps the meal, service or experience, and the creator posts 1 post or reel plus 1 story that shares the post, with the goal of posting within 48 hours of the visit (one week is the hard deadline), honestly and with clear gifted disclosure. On paid bookings the creator is booked at their own listed rate from $50 and keeps 97 percent of it.

What does it cost a Miami business?

Businesses apply free today and hold their category spot in application order. When the market opens, founder pricing is Self-Service $199 a month for 5 creator visits, Growth $349 a month for 10 creator visits, or Managed $699 a month for 20 creator visits, with $100 off the first month, a 3-month minimum, unused visits rolling one month then expiring, and founder rates kept while the plan stays active. Nothing is charged until the market opens.

What happens if a creator does not post?

The visit does not count against the business and it is replaced. That is the Visit Guarantee: credits, not cash, and it applies on every plan. Repeat no-shows are reviewed and strikes at three trigger a manual review.

What can a Miami business do with a creator’s post?

On a comped visit, the business may reshare the post natively with credit to the creator, and nothing more: no downloads, no reposting the file, no ad use. A paid booking includes one standard license, 90 days of repost and paid ad use, and a comped post can be licensed separately at the creator’s listed fee for 90 days, with the creator approving each use. The creator always owns the content.
