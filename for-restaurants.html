How it works

How SIGNL works here

/ 01

Post the comp

Offer what you would happily serve: dinner for two, a chef's counter tasting, a brunch spread. You set the value and how many visits you want this month.

/ 02

Creators visit like real guests

Local creators claim the offer, book through the app and redeem the comp inside the app on a single-use code. Your register and your point of sale are never touched.

/ 03

The post lands in 48 hours*

A tracked Standard Visit about the real visit: 1 post or reel plus 1 story that shares the post, with honest words and full disclosure. If a week passes with no post, the visit is replaced and does not count against your plan.

*Creators have up to one week to post. 48 hours is the standard they are held to, and hitting it earns their Momentum. If a week passes with no post, the visit is replaced and does not count against your plan.

Honest FAQ

Straight answers, no sales talk

Does influencer marketing work for restaurants?

Yes, and restaurants are SIGNL's core category, because restaurant marketing runs on word of mouth and a comped meal is a natural offer. Local creators visit like real guests, post honestly within 48 hours, and their followers are people who can actually book a table this week.

What does the comp actually cost my restaurant?

Whatever you decide to offer: a meal for two, a chef's counter seat, a tasting menu. You set the comp and its ceiling on every offer, and creators opt in knowing exactly what it is. The comp costs you your cost, not its menu price: a $100 dinner costs a restaurant its food cost, not $100. The comp is your own product, provided by you, and SIGNL never touches comp money: no pass-through, no markup, and no per-creator cash fees on standard visits.

Can cafes, bakeries and food trucks use SIGNL too?

Yes. Cafes and drinks-to-go businesses are some of the strongest fits on the platform: the comp costs little, and a coffee or juice offer keeps your name in local feeds week after week. Food trucks post their location and hours through creator visits the same way.

How much does it cost?

Published pricing: $199 a month for Self-Service (up to 5 creator visits a month, about $40 per creator visit), $349 a month for Growth (up to 10 creator visits a month, about $35 per creator visit) or $699 a month for Managed (up to 20 creator visits a month), with a 3-month minimum, then month to month. Your only other cost is the comp you choose to offer, provided at your cost, not retail. There are no per-creator cash fees on standard visits.

What if a creator does not post?

Creators post to a 48-hour standard, and on-time posts earn them Momentum. If no post has appeared within a week of the visit, you get a replacement visit and the slot does not count against your cap. Creators who no-show or do not post lose standing, and repeat offenders are removed from the roster entirely.

Do creators have to post something positive?

Never. Creators post their honest experience, and no business on SIGNL may require a positive post or review. That honesty is exactly what makes the posts believable to the people reading them. Businesses that deliver a good experience get good posts.
