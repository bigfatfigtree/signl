Creator FAQ

Questions, answered straight

I do not think of myself as an influencer. Is this for me?

That is the point. SIGNL's Neighbors band starts at 500 followers and exists for regular people whose friends trust their calls on wings, barbers, golf and game day. You do not need a ring light or a content calendar. If your group chat asks you where to go, you are the creator local businesses want.

What kinds of places comp dads?

The places in a dad's actual rotation: sports bars and BBQ joints, breweries and taprooms, barbershops, golf simulators, steak and burger spots, coffee shops and auto detailing, plus the family restaurants and weekend attractions you post about with your kids. Every offer states what is comped and how many people it covers before you claim it.

Can I bring a friend, or my kid?

Every offer states up front how many people the comp covers: just you, or you plus one. Plenty of offers cover two, which makes them a standing night out or a weekend post with your kid. If an offer covers one and you want company, your guest just orders like any other customer.

What exactly do I have to post, and how fast?

On every comped visit you post the Standard Visit: 1 post or reel plus 1 story that shares the post about the real visit, posted to a 48-hour standard, with up to a week at the outside, with a clear disclosure that the visit was comped. Honest is the whole point: no business can require a positive post, ever. Nobody can make you say you loved something you did not.

Does it cost anything to join, and can I get paid?

No. SIGNL is free for creators. Always. Businesses pay the platform. You never pay to join, to list, or to get booked. On standard visits your compensation is the comp itself: the meal, service or experience the business provides. The only cut SIGNL ever takes is 3 percent of a paid rate you set yourself, if a business books you at it. You keep the other 97 percent.
