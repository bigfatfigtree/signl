Compare platforms

Magpie vs the alternatives.

If you are comparing influencer platforms for a local business, you are probably looking at some of the same names we did: Storytime, Mustard, Momfluence, Cohley, Aspire, Insense and Collabstr. Here is what each one is, who it is built for, what it costs, and where Magpie is genuinely different: Magpie is a local influencer marketing platform for small businesses and local creators, built city by city, not a national platform with a location filter.

How to read this page

An honest comparison, with the receipts

Several of these are good tools for the businesses they were built for. The question is never "which platform is best" in the abstract. It is which one was built for a business like yours, in a city like yours, at a price you can read without booking a sales call.

Sources and dating

Competitor prices and terms are as published by each company or reported by trade press in October 2026, and every platform changes its pricing, so check their pages before you decide. Where a figure comes from reporting rather than the company's own page, this page says so. Names are used only to compare; no platform listed here is affiliated with Magpie, and Magpie is not affiliated with any of them.

Local creator visits · New York area

Storytime

Storytime is the closest model to Magpie anywhere: brick-and-mortar businesses pay a flat monthly fee, vetted local creators activate tiered offers, visit in person, and post within 48 hours while the platform tracks the posts. It is built for local businesses in the cities where it operates.

StorytimeMagpie
The model
Creators activate a voucher worth $15 or $25 depending on their reach tier, and validate it at your register during the visit.
Creators redeem a single-use code inside the Magpie app. Your register and your point of sale are never touched, and your staff validate nothing.
The audience
Creators are vetted on local reach, which is the right idea, but the vetting method is not published and businesses do not see a local audience percentage for each creator.
Every creator profile shows the share of that creator's followers who live in your market, labeled by source: Self-reported today; Verified by Instagram arrives when the Instagram connection activates. Profiles without enough data say so.
The price
Trade coverage in 2025 reported pricing from $150 a month for 15 creators a month. As of October 2026, Storytime's website does not publish prices and asks you to request a demo for pricing in your location.
Published on this website: $199 a month Self-Service, $349 a month Growth or $699 a month Managed, 3-month minimum. No demo call stands between you and the numbers.
The map
Operates in New York City, parts of New Jersey and Miami, with Boston in early access. No presence in Virginia or North Carolina.
Forming founding rosters now in 45 markets across 13 countries.

Choose Storytime if your business is in New York City, New Jersey or Miami and you want a local creator program that is already operating there.

Choose Magpie if you are in one of Magpie's cities and you want published pricing, per-creator local audience data before you choose, and redemption that stays off your register.

Restaurants only · Automated

Mustard

Mustard is an automated platform for restaurants only: the restaurant sets a food credit and campaign details, and the software invites creators, generates briefs, tracks posts, and reports results in a live dashboard.

MustardMagpie
The model
Fully automated on the base plan, with no dedicated human support. Creators get unique promo codes and tracking links, and the restaurant supplies the food credit.
Three ways to run it: Self-Service software you operate yourself, Growth with up to 10 visits a month, or Managed, where a human coordinator picks creators, books visits, chases posts and reports to you monthly.
The visits
The published base plan includes 3 influencer matches and hosted visits per month.
Self-Service includes up to 5 creator visits per month, about $40 per visit at the full allowance, with extra visits at $25 to $49 each.
The price
$299 a month as published in October 2026, with no contracts.
$199 a month Self-Service or $349 a month Growth, each with a 3-month minimum, then month to month, or $699 a month Managed. The minimum exists because creator marketing compounds over a season; one month is a coupon.
Who it is for
Restaurants only.
Restaurants and bars, plus salons, barbershops, fitness studios, retail, attractions and local services: any business people walk into.

Choose Mustard if you run a restaurant, 3 automated visits a month is enough, and you want zero human involvement.

Choose Magpie if you want more visits per month, a business type beyond restaurants, a human-run option, and local audience percentages on every creator profile.

Mom creators · National product brands

Momfluence

Momfluence connects brands with a large vetted roster of mom creators, run by its team as a hybrid agency: the brand ships product to creators, creators post about it, and the platform manages the campaign and reports. It is built for brands selling products to moms, nationally.

MomfluenceMagpie
The model
Per-campaign flat packages with no subscription or contract. As published in October 2026: $2,000 per campaign for 10 creators with product provided, $3,250 for a paid version with influencer fees included, and $5,000 for 30 creators.
A published subscription: $199, $349 or $699 a month. Local creators visit your business in person instead of receiving shipped product at home.
The math
The packages work out to roughly $167 to $325 per creator, depending on the package, with the product itself on top for the product-based tiers.
About $40 per creator visit at the full 5 visits on Self-Service. The comp you choose is the only cost beyond the subscription; there are no per-creator cash fees on standard visits.
The audience
Targeting is demographic, not geographic: moms, anywhere in the country. There is no local audience measure and no in-person visit anywhere in the model.
Targeting is geographic first: a vetted roster in your city, with each creator's local audience share shown on their profile with its source.
The content
Full content rights for multi-channel use are included in the campaign price.
Every delivered post is saved to your content library with its rights stated plainly: native share on comp visits; a 90-day repost and ad license on paid visits. Posts are honest by rule, never scripted positives.

Choose Momfluence if you sell a product to moms anywhere in the country and can ship it to creators.

Choose Magpie if your customers walk through your door, and the people you need posting are the ones who live near it.

Enterprise creator content · National brands

Cohley

Cohley is a creator content platform for mid-market and enterprise brands selling physical products. Brands post briefs to a large vetted creator pool, and the deliverable is content itself: photos, video and product reviews the brand uses across its own channels. By Cohley's own description, it is not built for local small businesses.

CohleyMagpie
The model
Content production at scale: an AI operations agent that runs briefs and matching, creators certified on FTC disclosure, automated product shipping to creators, and reviews syndicated to thousands of retail sites.
Creator visits at local scale: real people walk into your business, post to their own local audiences within 48 hours, and every visit and post is tracked in your dashboard.
The price
Not published; custom quotes, with creator compensation on top. A rival platform claims a starter package around $18,000; treat that figure with skepticism, but the fair summary as of October 2026 is five-figure annual entry in practice.
Published on this website: $199, $349 or $699 a month, 3-month minimum. A local business can read the entire price list in about a minute, because it is on the page.
The rights
Full usage rights in perpetuity are included in every asset. That is Cohley's headline offer, and for a national product brand it is a strong one.
Delivered posts are saved to your content library with clear, visit-stated reuse rights: native share on comp visits; a 90-day repost and ad license on paid visits. Creators post in their own voice to their own followers, and no post is ever required to be positive.
Who it is for
Mid-market and enterprise product brands, and the agencies that service them.
Local small and medium businesses: restaurants, salons, gyms, shops, attractions and services in one city.

Choose Cohley if you are a product brand that needs a continuous supply of content assets and has a budget in the five figures to run it.

Choose Magpie if your marketing problem is quiet weekdays in one city, not content volume for a national catalog.

Enterprise influencer CRM · Marketing teams

Aspire

Aspire is a full influencer program platform for larger brands: a large creator discovery database and inbound marketplace, CRM, contracts, product gifting, affiliate tracking and sales attribution. It is the reference for what an enterprise creator stack looks like.

AspireMagpie
The price
Not published. Third-party reviews in October 2026 report the Essentials tier at around $2,299 a month on a 12-month contract, plus a one-time onboarding fee of about $2,000.
Published pricing, a 3-month minimum, then month to month. No onboarding fee and no annual lock-in.
The work
Your team runs the program inside the software: discovery, outreach, contracts, approvals, payments. The platform is the tool; the labor is yours.
Run it yourself in minutes a week on Self-Service, or take Managed and a coordinator handles selection, scheduling, chasing and reporting for you.
The audience
First-party audience data from the social platforms, presented inside an analytics stack aimed at national programs.
One number, front and center on every creator profile: the share of that creator's followers who live in your market, with the source printed next to it.
Who it is for
Brands with a marketing department running creator programs at national scale.
Owners of local businesses who are, in practice, the marketing department.

Choose Aspire if you have staff whose job is running creator programs, and a national program to run.

Choose Magpie if you are an owner in one city and want local creators handled as a service, not adopted as a software project.

UGC and influencer subscriptions · Global pool

Insense

Insense is a subscription platform for UGC and influencer posts: brands post briefs, creators from a global pool apply, and the platform handles payments and delivery. A managed, done-for-you service is sold on top.

InsenseMagpie
The price
As published in October 2026: the Brand plan is $400 a month billed annually or $500 a month billed quarterly, plus a marketplace fee of 10 percent on every creator payment. Creator payments are extra: influencer posts start around $125 for nano creators and UGC videos around $100.
$199, $349 or $699 a month, published, with no percentage taken on creator payments. On standard visits there are no per-creator cash fees at all, because the comp is the payment model.
The human option
A dedicated platform manager starts at $1,600 a month; managed content packages run higher.
The Managed plan, a human coordinator who runs your local program end to end, is $699 a month.
The audience
A global pool across 35+ countries. Audience insights are shown for creators who apply to your brief.
A hand-built roster in your city only, with local audience share on every profile before you send a single offer.
The rights
Perpetual digital usage rights are included on commissioned content.
Delivered posts are saved to your content library with visit-based rights (native share on comp visits; a 90-day repost and ad license on paid visits), and every post is the creator's honest take, never a scripted positive.

Choose Insense if you need UGC video ads for e-commerce and want a global pool of creators applying to your briefs.

Choose Magpie if you need people inside your actual business, posting to audiences that can actually visit you.

Self-serve marketplace · Hire creators yourself

Collabstr

Collabstr is a marketplace where creators list profiles with their own rates. Brands search and filter, hire, and pay through escrow, or post a campaign and take applications. The platform provides the rails; the work is the brand's.

CollabstrMagpie
The model
You do everything: searching, vetting, negotiating rates, briefing, approving. Collabstr provides chat, contracts, escrow and post tracking.
A vetted local roster with offers, booking, single-use visit codes and post tracking built in. On the Managed plan, a coordinator does the work.
The price
Free to browse with a 10 percent hiring fee on every order. Pro is $249 a month billed annually ($299 monthly) plus the same 10 percent hiring fee, as published in October 2026. Creator rates are on top of both.
A flat published subscription: $199, $349 or $699 a month. No hiring fees and no per-creator rates on standard visits, because creators are paid in the comp you offer.
The audience
You can filter by the creator's own location; audience demographic reports are a paid-plan feature. Where a creator lives and where their followers live are different numbers, and only one of them is on the profile.
Local audience share is displayed on every profile with its source, so the number you are choosing on is the followers in your market, not the creator's home address.
The visit
Nothing verifies an in-person visit, because the model is built for content delivered online.
Every visit is verified by a single-use code redeemed in the Magpie app, creators post to a 48-hour standard, and a post that never lands earns you a replacement visit.

Choose Collabstr if you want to hire individual creators for cash, one brief at a time, and you enjoy running the process yourself.

Choose Magpie if you want a standing local roster, comp-based visits verified in person, and enforcement handled when a post goes missing.

Comparison FAQ

The questions people actually ask

What is the best Storytime alternative for a local business?

It depends on your map. Storytime runs in New York City, parts of New Jersey and Miami. If your business is in one of Magpie's rollout cities (from Hampton Roads and Washington DC to London and Dubai), Magpie is the direct alternative built on the same local-visit idea, with three differences: pricing is published ($199, $349 or $699 a month, as of October 2026), every creator profile shows the local audience percentage with its source, and comps redeem inside the app instead of at your register. If you are in Storytime's cities and want a program already operating there, Storytime is the honest pick.

Is there a Cohley alternative without enterprise pricing?

Yes, if what you need is local creator marketing rather than a content factory. Cohley is built for mid-market and enterprise product brands, with custom pricing that a rival platform puts around $18,000 to start, as reported in October 2026. Magpie publishes its pricing: $199 a month Self-Service, $349 a month Growth or $699 a month Managed, built for local businesses whose creators visit in person and post to local audiences.

What is the best influencer marketing platform for restaurants?

For a single restaurant or a local group, the realistic shortlist is local-first tools: Mustard (restaurants only, automated, $299 a month with 3 visits included, as of October 2026), Storytime if you are in its cities, and Magpie, which covers restaurants and bars plus other local businesses, includes up to 5 visits a month on Self-Service, shows each creator's local audience share, and offers a human Managed tier. National platforms like Aspire and Cohley are built for brands with marketing teams and budgets to match.

How much does local influencer marketing cost?

On Magpie, $199 a month for Self-Service (up to 5 creator visits a month, about $40 per visit), $349 a month for Growth (up to 10 visits a month, about $35 per visit) or $699 a month for Managed (up to 20 visits a month), with a 3-month minimum, plus the comp you choose to offer. Across the field, as of October 2026: Storytime was reported from $150 a month in 2025 trade coverage and no longer publishes prices; Mustard is $299 a month for 3 visits; Momfluence campaigns start at $2,000 for 10 creators; enterprise platforms run from a reported $2,299 a month (Aspire) to five-figure custom quotes (Cohley).

Storytime vs Magpie: what is the difference?

Both connect local businesses with vetted local creators who visit and post within 48 hours. The differences: Storytime operates in New York City, parts of New Jersey and Miami and does not publish current pricing; Magpie is forming rosters in 45 founding markets across 13 countries and publishes $199, $349 and $699 pricing. Storytime creators validate tiered vouchers at your register; Magpie codes redeem inside the app. Storytime vets creators on local reach privately; Magpie shows each creator's local audience percentage on their profile, labeled by source.

Is there a Mustard alternative that works for more than restaurants?

Yes. Mustard is restaurants only. Magpie runs the same visit-and-post model for restaurants and bars, salons and barbershops, fitness studios, retail, attractions and local services, because its roster is built per city across categories. Magpie also includes up to 5 visits a month on Self-Service at $199 a month, versus Mustard's 3 visits on its $199 a month base plan (as of October 2026), and adds a Growth tier at $349 a month for up to 10 visits plus a Managed tier at $699 a month with a human coordinator.

What is the best micro-influencer platform for a small business?

Look for three things: creators whose audiences are actually in your market, a price you can read without a sales call, and enforcement when a post does not happen. Magpie is built on exactly those: a Neighbors tier of everyday locals (500 to 2,000 followers) and Locals (2,000 to 15,000), local audience share on every profile, published pricing, and a replacement visit if a week passes with no post.

Collabstr vs Magpie: which is better for a local business?

They are different tools. Collabstr is a do-it-yourself marketplace: you search, negotiate and pay creators cash per job, with a 10 percent hiring fee; its Pro plan is $249 to $199 a month as of October 2026. Magpie is a managed local system: a vetted roster in your city, comp-based visits verified by in-app codes, posts tracked to a 48-hour standard, and replacements when a creator flakes. If you want one-off paid content and like running the process, Collabstr fits. If you want local regulars posting about your business every month, Magpie fits.
