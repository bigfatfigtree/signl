For creators: the deal, up front

SIGNL is free for creators. Always. Businesses pay the platform. You never pay to join, to list, or to get booked.

Comp visits are the core. Paid bookings sit on top: set your own rate, the business pays it, and the platform fee is 6 percent total, split evenly, so you keep 97 percent of your rate. Paid bookings are held by SIGNL until your post is live. Headliners (15,000+ followers) are also first in line for featured paid campaigns when businesses run them.

Why San Diego, why now

Seventy-two and sunny, and hungry

The Cali burrito debate

Fries inside the burrito: San Diego put them there, and every neighborhood has a ruling on whose carne asada fries set the standard. Food debates this deep are creator gold.

Beer worth a pilgrimage

San Diego County's craft beer scene is a genuine landmark, and its taprooms are neighborhood institutions from North Park to Vista. Institutions with regulars are where comps work hardest.

Border flavors, local pride

Convoy's pan-Asian food row and Barrio Logan's Chicano food culture give San Diego layers most cities cannot copy. Creators here post across all of them, fluently.

Outdoor everything

Beach mornings, canyon hikes, patio dinners in January: the San Diego feed never goes dark, and neither does its appetite for the next local spot.

For San Diego businesses

San Diego businesses: locals over tourists, daily

San Diego's visitor economy is famous, but its survivor businesses run on locals in every month of the year. Founding businesses get the creators those locals follow: comped at your cost, posting honestly, tracked from visit to post.

Ads reach everyone except the people who live nearby. SIGNL puts San Diego creators who live here inside your business, posting to neighbors who can walk in this week.

/ 01

Join the founding business list

Apply in a few minutes. Your category spot in your trade area is held in application order, and nothing is charged today.

/ 02

Founding pricing, held for you

$199 a month for Self-Service (up to 5 creator visits a month, about $40 per creator visit), $349 a month for Growth (up to 10 creator visits a month, about $35 per creator visit) or $699 a month for Managed (up to 20 creator visits a month), with a 3-month minimum and then month to month. Founding businesses get $100 off the first month. Visits you don't use roll into the next month, then expire. The only other cost is the comp the business chooses to offer, provided at its cost, not retail. Founding businesses keep founding pricing while they remain subscribed, and the plan begins when the San Diego market opens.

/ 03

Activate when San Diego opens

Pick creators from the founding roster, offer a comp at your cost, and get honest, tracked posts delivered in 48 hours* of each visit.

*Creators have up to one week to post. 48 hours is the standard they are held to, and hitting it earns their Momentum. If a week passes with no post, the visit is replaced and does not count against your plan.

The Visit Guarantee

If a booked creator does not show up or does not post, that visit does not count against your monthly visits. It is replaced.

How content rights work: on a comp visit, you can share the creator's post natively with the creator credited, and nothing more: no downloads, no reposting the file, no ads. On a paid visit, you can also repost the content on your own channels and run it as an ad for 90 days under the standard license included in the booking. The creator always owns the content. Rights follow the visit, never the plan.
