For creators: the deal, up front

SIGNL is free for creators. Always. Businesses pay the platform. You never pay to join, to list, or to get booked.

Comp visits are the core. Paid bookings sit on top: set your own rate, the business pays it, and the platform fee is 6 percent total, split evenly, so you keep 97 percent of your rate. Paid bookings are held by SIGNL until your post is live. Headliners (15,000+ followers) are also first in line for paid hero campaigns when businesses run them.

Why Philadelphia should open

BYOB city, sandwich city

The BYOB economy

Hundreds of small Philly restaurants skip the liquor license and let you bring your own bottle, which keeps rooms small, prices sane and regulars loyal. At a BYOB, a comped meal is the whole evening, and a creator's post about it carries the room.

Roast pork truth

The cheesesteak is famous. The roast pork is better, and Philly's willingness to argue the point in public is exactly the engagement local businesses dream about.

Fishtown and the northern tier

Fishtown, Northern Liberties and Kensington turned warehouses into the city's creative dining map, while Manayunk and Passyunk keep their own loyal orbits. Every orbit is a roster neighborhood.

Block-level loyalty

Philly might be the most neighborhood-proud big city in America. People do not just live in South Philly or Fishtown, they represent them. Representation is the whole product here.

Tiers are set automatically by your total audience across platforms, and they rise as you grow. A small, genuinely local audience beats a big, scattered one.

Philadelphia FAQ

Questions, answered straight

Is SIGNL open in Philadelphia yet?

No, and we will not pretend otherwise. Philadelphia is not one of SIGNL's 39 founding markets yet, so there are no offers and no roster there today. Sign-ups from Philadelphia are counted city by city, and the cities with the most sign-ups open first. Sign up now and you are first on the list when Philadelphia opens.

How do I sign up if Philadelphia is not listed?

In the SIGNL app, choose My city is not listed yet and type Philadelphia. That is a real sign-up, counted toward opening Philadelphia, and it puts you at the front of its founding list if the city opens. It takes about two minutes.

Does it cost anything to join SIGNL as a creator?

No. SIGNL is free for creators. Always. Businesses pay the platform. You never pay to join, to list, or to get booked. Your compensation on standard visits is the comp itself: the meal, service or experience the business provides.
