For creators: the deal, up front

SIGNL is free for creators. Always. Businesses pay the platform. You never pay to join, to list, or to get booked.

Comp visits are the core. Paid bookings sit on top: set your own rate, the business pays it, and the platform fee is 6 percent total, split evenly, so you keep 97 percent of your rate. Paid bookings are held by SIGNL until your post is live. Headliners (15,000+ followers) are also first in line for paid hero campaigns when businesses run them.

Why Pittsburgh should open

Reinvention, with pierogies

Fries on the sandwich

Pittsburgh's signature move, fries and slaw inside the sandwich, started as a truck driver's lunch and became a civic identity. Cities with food identities this strong grow creator rosters fast.

The Strip is a weekly ritual

Saturday in the Strip District, with its markets, street vendors and old-school counters, is how Pittsburgh shops and eats at the same time. Rituals are content engines.

Steel grit, tech energy

Robotics, medicine and universities rebuilt the economy while the neighborhoods kept their accents and their taverns. That mix of old and new is a feed that never repeats itself.

Ninety neighborhoods, give or take

Pittsburgh counts its neighborhoods in the dozens and defends each one. Lawrenceville is not Shadyside is not the South Side, and locals will correct you. Hyperlocal is home turf here.

Creator tiers

Where do you land?

Tiers are set automatically by your total audience across platforms, and they rise as you grow. A small, genuinely local audience beats a big, scattered one.

Neighbors
500 – 2k followers
  • Everyday people with a local following
  • Comp only
  • Perfect first step into local posting
Locals
2k – 15k followers
  • Regular local posters, the roster's sweet spot
  • Comp plus roster perks
  • Priority access to new businesses
Headliners
15k+ followers
  • Established local creators
  • Comp plus eligibility for paid hero campaigns
  • First pick of grand openings and launches

Under 500 followers, or just starting out? Apply anyway. Your tier is set by your follower count, and it moves up as you grow. What matters most is not the number, it's that your audience is actually local and actually engages. Pick your band honestly when you sign up.

Pittsburgh FAQ

Questions, answered straight

Is SIGNL open in Pittsburgh yet?

No, and we will not pretend otherwise. Pittsburgh is not one of SIGNL's 39 founding markets yet, so there are no offers and no roster there today. Sign-ups from Pittsburgh are counted city by city, and the cities with the most sign-ups open first. Sign up now and you are first on the list when Pittsburgh opens.

How do I sign up if Pittsburgh is not listed?

In the SIGNL app, choose My city is not listed yet and type Pittsburgh. That is a real sign-up, counted toward opening Pittsburgh, and it puts you at the front of its founding list if the city opens. It takes about two minutes.

Does it cost anything to join SIGNL as a creator?

No. SIGNL is free for creators. Always. Businesses pay the platform. You never pay to join, to list, or to get booked. Your compensation on standard visits is the comp itself: the meal, service or experience the business provides.

What would I actually post about in Pittsburgh?

A fries-inside ruling at your counter, a Strip District Saturday, a Butler Street crawl, pierogies done right. The Standard Visit is 1 post or reel plus 2 stories, live within 48 hours of the visit, honest and clearly disclosed as comped.

I am a student at Pitt or Carnegie Mellon. Can I join?

Yes. Name your school when you sign up or finish your profile and your profile carries your campus, so businesses near your school can find you first. The Standard Visit fits between classes, and alcohol offers are 21+ only.
