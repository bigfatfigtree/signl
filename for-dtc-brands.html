Straight talk before anything else

Magpie is not a pure UGC mill. If what you need is a high volume of anonymous ad creative, shot to a script, with perpetual rights, that is a different tool and you should buy that tool. What Magpie does is put your product in the hands of real local creators whose followers trust them, and give you creator-shot content from paid bookings under a 90-day license, never perpetuity. Read the fit questions in the FAQ below before you sign up. We would rather lose the signup than sell you the wrong thing.

How it works

From your warehouse to their city

/ 01

Ship the product to the creator

You seed the product and ship it to the creator's home. The terms sit on one card both sides confirm first: what posts, by when, what rights, when money moves. The creator knows exactly what they agreed to before the box arrives.

/ 02

The creator shoots and posts from their city

The creator uses the product in their real home and routine, shoots it themselves, and posts to their own audience in their own city. It reads as a neighbor's honest post because that is what it is, not as a studio ad.

/ 03

The post lands in 48 hours*

A tracked booking: 1 post or reel plus 1 story that shares the post, honestly told and fully disclosed. Delivered posts are saved to your content library. If a week passes with no post, the booking is replaced and does not count against your plan.

*Creators have up to one week to post. The goal is to post within 48 hours of the visit, and hitting it earns their Momentum. If a week passes with no post, the booking is replaced and does not count against your plan.

By category

Where local creators fit, and where they do not

Place is the product on Magpie, so the honest answer changes by category. Here is the fit, category by category, including the places we will talk you out of.

/ 01

Beauty

A strong fit. Beauty is routine content, and routines happen in real bathrooms and bedrooms in real cities. Creators post their personal experience with your product inside their own routine. The exception is claim-led products: if the pitch needs treatment claims to work, an honest creator post cannot carry it.

/ 02

Apparel

A fit, with eyes open. Creators wear the piece in their own city, styled their own way, which is exactly why their followers believe it. If you need precise styling control, exact color grading and a script, that is a studio shoot, and you should book one instead.

/ 03

Supplements and wellness

A careful fit. Creators can share how a product fits their routine, in their own words, as personal experience only. They cannot make health or treatment claims, and Magpie does not let brands ask. Brands that sell on claims should not start here.

/ 04

Home goods

One of the strongest fits on this page. Home products need a home, and a creator's real kitchen, desk or living room beats a styled set for believability. The post runs to people nearby who are furnishing the same kind of rooms.

/ 05

Pet

A strong fit. Pet creators are trusted the way good neighbors are trusted, and the product test is honest by nature: the pet votes on camera. Food, treats, toys and care products all travel well in this format.

/ 06

Food and drink

A fit for shelf stable and shippable products: snacks, sauces, coffee, drink mixes, subscription boxes. The unboxing and the first taste happen in a real kitchen, in a named city. Perishables depend on your shipping, and if your cold chain is shaky, fix that before you seed creators.

Honest FAQ

Questions, answered straight

How is this different from a national influencer platform?

National platforms and UGC marketplaces optimize for volume: the most content, from the most creators, anywhere. Magpie optimizes for place. Creators are ranked by local reach in their market, the product is shot in a real home in a named city, and the post runs to an audience that is concentrated there. If place does not matter to your brand, the national platforms are the honest better fit. If it does, that concentration is the whole point.

Is Magpie a UGC platform?

Not a pure one, and we will not pretend otherwise. Most DTC demand for creator content is a steady supply of creator-shot video to run as ads. Magpie bookings start as real posts to a real local audience, and paid bookings give you creator content with a 90-day license for reposting and ads. What Magpie does not sell is anonymous, scripted, perpetual-rights content by the batch. Brands that need that should use a UGC marketplace. Brands that want trusted local voices, with usable content as part of the deal, are who this page is for.

What usage rights do we get?

On a standard booking you can share the delivered post natively, with the creator credited. Paid bookings carry the standard license: repost rights plus ad use of the creator's content for 90 days. Never perpetuity. If you need the content for longer, you renew the license with the creator rather than owning the post forever.

How much does it cost?

Published founding pricing: $199 a month for Self-Service (up to 5 creator bookings a month, about $40 per booking), $349 a month for Growth (up to 10 bookings, about $35 per booking), or $699 a month for Managed (up to 20 bookings). Signing up includes $100 off each of your first 3 months, and your founding rate is held while your plan stays active. Plans carry a 3-month minimum once active, then run month to month. Your only other costs are the product you seed and any paid bookings you choose, at each creator's own listed rate. There are no per-creator cash fees on standard bookings.

When do we pay?

Not today. No Magpie market is live for money yet. Signing up locks your founding price and holds your spot in application order, and markets activate one by one as each founding creator roster completes. Your plan, and your billing, begin when the first market in your program activates. Nothing is charged before that.

How do we know it worked?

You see what Magpie can count, counted: posts live, the content delivered to your library, and store check-ins where a booking includes a store visit, tracked market by market. Magpie does not see your store analytics or your ad account, and it will not invent attribution it cannot measure. You read the tracked posts alongside your own sales and ad data, which stay yours.

Do creators have to post something positive?

Never. Creators post their honest personal experience, and no brand on Magpie may require a positive post or review. Every post carries full disclosure that the product was provided or the post was paid. For beauty and household products, posts stay personal experiences only, with no health or treatment claims. That honesty is exactly what makes the posts believable to the people reading them.

How does product seeding work on Magpie?

Product seeding with micro-influencers is the comp model here: you ship the product to the creator, they use it at home and post honestly about it, and the booking is tracked like any other. Seeding is agreed on the booking card before anything ships, so the creator knows the deliverable and you know the post is owed. If you would rather pay, book the creator paid at their listed rate instead.

How is this different from a UGC platform?

UGC platforms for ecommerce brands sell you content files and rights, optimized for ad creative volume. Magpie sells you a post: a real creator publishing to their own local audience, with the content saved to your library and a 90-day license on paid bookings. If you need fifty ad variants this month, buy the UGC platform. If you need people in specific cities to trust the product, that is this page.

Can we whitelist a post or run it as a Spark Ad?

The standard license covers sharing the post natively and running the content as your brand's ad creative for 90 days. Running ads from the creator's own handle, whitelisting or Spark, is not part of the standard license. If that is central to your plan, say so when we scope your program and we will tell you plainly what is possible.

What happens if a creator does not post?

The booking is replaced and does not count against your plan. Creators aim to post within 48 hours of receiving the product, and the outside limit is one week. A creator who takes the product and goes quiet is not a cost you absorb.

Can we start in one city?

Yes. A one city pilot on the founding plan is the sensible first step for most online brands: pick the city where your customers, or your retail ambitions, already concentrate, run the seeding play there, and read the counted posts before you widen it.

Do you charge per post or take a cut of sales?

Neither. You pay the founding plan price, the product you seed, and any paid bookings you choose at each creator's listed rate, of which the creator keeps 97 percent. There are no per-creator cash fees on standard bookings and no commission on your sales.
