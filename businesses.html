Creator matching

Matched on fit, not fame.

Most platforms hand you a list sorted by follower count and call it discovery. When offers open in your city, SIGNL scores every creator on the roster against your business: what they actually post, whether it matches your category, your neighborhood and city, how local their audience is, and the record they are building. The score sits on the card with its reasons written out in plain words, so you always know why someone is in front of you.

Fame is not one of the signals. A smaller creator who posts exactly your kind of place, in your neighborhood, to people who live there, can outrank a bigger account that does none of that. That is the match that fills tables.

Sort the roster by Best fit when you want the ranking working for you. The default order stays founding order, first come first, and the score never hides a creator from you.

Honest FAQ

Straight answers, no sales talk

Is SIGNL the cheapest way to hire local creators?

On total cost, yes, and here is the math. The smallest creators average about $200 a post, and once a creator has a manager the quote commonly runs $1,000 a post or more. A PR firm runs thousands a month whether anyone posts or not. On SIGNL, Self-Service is $299 a month for up to 8 creator visits, about $37 a visit, and the visit itself is comped with your product at your cost, not its menu price. Every creator is local to your market, which no national platform can sell you at any price.

How is this different from other influencer platforms?

Most influencer platforms sell national reach to brands with ad teams, and local tools sell discount vouchers redeemed at your register. SIGNL does neither. You browse a vetted roster of people who live in your city, you see each creator's local audience share before you choose, and comps redeem inside the app instead of at your point of sale. Posts run on a 48-hour deadline with a replacement guarantee, pricing is published on this page, and nobody is ever required to post positively.

How many creators will come out?

Honest answer: we guarantee tracked posts and deliverables, not a headcount. Anyone who promises you an exact number of creators is guessing. What you control is your monthly visit allowance (8 on Self-Service, 20 on Growth, 30 on Managed); what we enforce is that every accepted visit produces a tracked post within 48 hours. Managed clients get a coordinator actively working the roster to fill their calendar, that's the closest thing to a guarantee that actually means something.

What if a creator doesn't post?

The 48-hour rule is the backbone of the platform. If a post doesn't appear within 48 hours of a visit, you get a replacement visit, the slot doesn't count against your cap. Creators who no-show or don't post lose standing, and repeat offenders are removed from the roster entirely. Their next free meal depends on your post existing.

What is the Visit Guarantee?

If a booked creator does not show up or does not post, that visit does not count against your monthly visits. It is replaced.

What is the refund policy?

Paid bookings open with the paid lane, and nothing is charged before then. When paid bookings are running, payment is held by SIGNL until the post is live, and if the visit does not happen or the post never lands, the business is refunded in full. If we cannot deliver the service, including a founding market that does not open, founding businesses receive a full refund of what they paid for that market. Plans run month to month after the 3 month minimum. Cancelling stops future billing. Visits already delivered are not refundable.

What does the comp actually cost me?

Whatever you decide to offer: a meal for two, a service, a package. You set the comp and its ceiling on every offer, and creators opt in knowing exactly what it is. The comp costs you your cost, not its retail price: a $100 dinner costs a restaurant its food cost, not $100. The comp is your own product, provided by you, and SIGNL never touches comp money: no pass-through, no markup, and no per-creator cash fees on standard visits.

Can creators trash my business?

Creators post their honest experience, that's what makes their posts believable and valuable. They can't be required to post positively, and neither can you require a good review anywhere else. In practice, businesses that deliver a good experience get good posts. If a post is factually false or violates platform rules, we review it with you.

Why the 3-month minimum?

Because one month of posts isn't a marketing strategy, it's a coupon. Creator marketing compounds: regulars see you repeatedly, in feeds they trust, over a season. Three months is the honest runway for that pattern to establish. After the first three months your plan runs month to month, and founding pricing stays locked while you remain subscribed.

How do I get creators to post about my business?

Post a comp offer: what you are offering (a meal for two, a service, a package), its value, and how many visits you want. Creators on your city's vetted roster claim the offer, visit like real customers, and post within 48 hours. Every post is tracked in your dashboard, and a missed post is replaced. On the Managed plan, a coordinator makes the matches and books the visits for you.

How does SIGNL decide which creators to show my business?

Every creator on your city's roster is scored against your business, and the reasons are printed on their card in plain words. The score weighs what they post and whether it matches your category, where they are based (your neighborhood and city count most), how local their audience is, and the record they have built delivering posts. Follower count is not one of the signals, so a smaller creator who is exactly right can outrank a bigger one who is not. You can sort the roster by Best fit whenever you want; the default order stays founding order, in the order creators joined, and the fit score never hides a creator from you.

How much does influencer marketing cost for a small business?

On SIGNL the price is published: $299 a month for Self-Service (up to 8 creator visits a month, about $37 per creator visit), $599 a month for Growth (up to 20 creator visits a month, about $30 per creator visit) or $999 a month for Managed (up to 30 creator visits a month), with a 3-month minimum. Your only other cost is the comp you choose to offer. For scale: national influencer platforms built for brands are commonly reported in the thousands per month on annual contracts, and managed agencies charge per campaign. Local creator marketing should not require a marketing department's budget.

Does influencer marketing work for restaurants?

Restaurants and bars are SIGNL's core category, because restaurant marketing runs on word of mouth and a comped meal is a natural offer. Local creators visit like real guests, post honestly within 48 hours, and their followers are people who can actually book a table this week. Coffee shops, bakeries and food trucks run the same play.

What is micro-influencer marketing, and is it right for a small business?

Micro-influencer marketing means working with smaller creators instead of big names. SIGNL's roster starts with Neighbors (500 to 2,000 followers) and Locals (2,000 to 15,000): people whose followers are concentrated in your city. For a local business, 2,000 followers who live ten minutes away beat 200,000 spread across the country, and SIGNL shows each creator's local audience share on their profile so you can see the number before you choose.

Can SIGNL work like a brand ambassador program for my small business?

Yes. In practice that is what the roster becomes: favorite the creators who fit your business, book them again with one tap, and build a standing set of regulars who post about you month after month. Managed clients get a coordinator who maintains that set for them: a brand ambassador program without the agency retainer.

Do I get UGC I can reuse in my own marketing?

Every delivered post is saved to your content library, so the content keeps working after the visit. What you may do with it follows the visit, not your plan: on a comp visit you can share the post natively, with the creator credited, and nothing more (no downloads, no reposting the file, no ads). On a paid visit you can also repost the post on your own channels (your social feeds, your website) and run it as an ad for 6 months from the post date. The creator always owns their content. It is UGC marketing with the tracking and the rights handled up front instead of negotiated afterward.

I already have a referral program. Does SIGNL replace it?

No. SIGNL feeds the program you already run. Paste your referral link or code into SIGNL once and it rides on every creator opportunity you post, so creators carry it in their posts, and the bookings it drives are tracked inside your own referral program, in the dashboard you already use. Your program keeps its rules and its payouts, and SIGNL never touches either one. What changes is the supply: instead of you hunting for people to hand your link to, a roster of local creators posts about your business with your link in their hands.

We have multiple locations. Do we pay per location?

No. One plan covers every location you run, and SIGNL never charges per location. Plans scale with creator visits per month, not with locations: up to 8 visits a month on Self-Service, 20 on Growth, and 30 on Managed, and those visits can be used across all your locations. Most multi-location businesses simply pick a higher tier because they want more creator visits each month, not because their locations force them to.
