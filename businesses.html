Honest FAQ

Straight answers, no sales talk

Is SIGNL the cheapest way to hire local creators?

On total cost, yes. Small creators average about $200 a post and managed quotes commonly run $1,000 or more. Self-Service is $299 a month for up to 8 visits, about $37 a visit, comped with your product at your cost. Every creator is local.

How is this different from other influencer platforms?

National platforms sell reach, local tools sell vouchers at your register. SIGNL shows local audience share before you choose, redeems comps in the app, tracks posts on a 48 hour deadline with a replacement guarantee, and never requires positive posts.

How many creators will come out?

We guarantee tracked posts, not a headcount. You control your visit allowance: 8 on Self-Service, 20 on Growth, 30 on Managed. Every accepted visit must produce a tracked post within 48 hours.

What if a creator doesn't post?

The 48-hour rule is the backbone of the platform. If a post does not appear within 48 hours of a visit, you get a replacement visit, the slot does not count against your cap. Creators who no show or do not post lose standing, and repeat offenders are removed from the roster entirely.

What is the Visit Guarantee?

If a booked creator does not show up or does not post, that visit does not count against your monthly visits. It is replaced.

What is the refund policy?

Paid bookings are held until the post is live and refunded in full if the visit or post never lands. If we cannot deliver, including a market that does not open, founding businesses get a full refund. Plans run month to month after the 3 month minimum.

What does the comp actually cost me?

Whatever you offer: a meal for two, a service, a package. You set the comp and ceiling. It costs your cost, not retail, and SIGNL never touches comp money.

Can creators trash my business?

Creators post honest experiences and cannot be required to post positively. In practice, good experiences get good posts. Factually false posts or rule violations are reviewed with you.

Why the 3-month minimum?

Because one month of posts is not a marketing strategy, it is a coupon. Creator marketing compounds over a season of trusted posts, and three months is the honest runway. After that, plans run month to month and founding pricing stays locked while subscribed.

How do I know it worked?

Your dashboard tracks every visit, every live post, estimated local reach, and redemptions of the trackable offer attached to each visit. See the sample dashboard for exactly what that looks like.

Can I create my business account today?

Not yet. Business accounts open as each market roster completes, over 30 to 90 days. Join the founding list to hold your category spot in order and lock founding pricing: $299, $599, or $999 a month.

How do I get creators to post about my business?

Post a comp offer with its value and visit count. Roster creators claim it, visit like real customers, and post within 48 hours. Missed posts are replaced. Managed includes a coordinator who books for you.

How does SIGNL decide which creators to show my business?

Every creator is scored against your business on content fit, neighborhood, local audience, and delivery record, with reasons on their card. Follower count is not a signal. Sort by Best fit or keep founding order.

How much does influencer marketing cost for a small business?

Published pricing: $299 a month Self-Service for up to 8 visits, $599 Growth for up to 20, $999 Managed for up to 30, with a 3 month minimum. Your only other cost is the comp you choose.

What types of businesses work with SIGNL?

Any business customers visit in person: restaurants, cafes, salons, fitness, retail, events, and health and aesthetic services. Health posts are personal experiences only, with disclosure and no treatment claims.

What types of businesses can use SIGNL?

Almost any local business people visit in person, across dining, cafes, nightlife, beauty, health, fitness, retail, experiences, hospitality, events, and visual services. If customers are local and it shows well on camera, it fits.

Does influencer marketing work for restaurants?

Yes. Restaurants and bars are the core category. Local creators visit like real guests and post within 48 hours to followers who can book this week.

What is micro-influencer marketing, and is it right for a small business?

It means smaller creators: Neighbors at 500 to 2,000 followers and Locals at 2,000 to 15,000, concentrated in your city. SIGNL shows each creator local audience share before you choose.

Can SIGNL work like a brand ambassador program for my small business?

Yes. Favorite creators who fit, rebook them with one tap, and build regulars who post monthly. Managed clients get a coordinator to maintain that set.

Do I get UGC I can reuse in my own marketing?

Delivered posts save to your content library. Comp visits are native share only, with credit. Paid visits add repost rights and ad use for 6 months from the post date. Creators own their content.

I already have a referral program. Does SIGNL replace it?

No. Paste your referral link or code once and it rides on every opportunity. Bookings track in your own program and dashboard. SIGNL never touches your rules or payouts.

We have multiple locations. Do we pay per location?

No. One plan covers every location. Plans scale by visits per month, 8, 20, or 30, usable across all locations.
