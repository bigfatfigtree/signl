How it works

How Magpie works here

/ 01

Post the comp

Offer what you would happily pour: a seasonal drink and a pastry, a baker's dozen preview, a flight of the new roasts. You set the value and how many visits you want this month. A cafe comp is the cheapest comp on the platform, and it costs you your cost, not its menu price.

/ 02

Creators visit like real customers

Local creators claim the offer, book through the app and redeem the comp inside the app on a single-use code. Your register and your point of sale are never touched, and the laptop crowd watches it happen in real time.

/ 03

The post lands in 48 hours*

A tracked Standard Visit about the real visit: 1 post or reel plus 1 story that shares the post, with honest words and full disclosure. Aim visits at your slow windows and new launches, and if a week passes with no post, the visit is replaced and does not count against your plan.

*Creators have up to one week to post. The goal is to post within 48 hours of the visit, and hitting it earns their Momentum. If a week passes with no post, the visit is replaced and does not count against your plan.

Honest FAQ

Questions, answered straight

Does influencer marketing work for cafes and bakeries?

Yes, and cafes are one of the strongest fits on the platform, because cafe marketing runs on habit and word of mouth. A comped drink and pastry costs you a few dollars at your cost, local creators post honestly, with the goal of posting within 48 hours of the visit, and their followers are people who can make your cafe their regular stop this week.

What does the comp actually cost my cafe?

Whatever you decide to offer: a seasonal drink, a pastry, a coffee flight. You set the comp and its ceiling on every offer, and creators opt in knowing exactly what it is. The comp costs you your cost, not its menu price: a $14 drink and pastry costs a cafe its ingredient cost, not $14. The comp is your own product, provided by you, and Magpie never touches comp money: no pass-through, no markup, and no per-creator cash fees on standard visits.

How much does it cost?

Published pricing: $199 a month for Self-Service (up to 5 creator visits a month, about $40 per creator visit), $349 a month for Growth (up to 10 creator visits a month, about $35 per creator visit) or $699 a month for Managed (up to 20 creator visits a month), with a 3-month minimum, then month to month. Your only other cost is the comp you choose to offer, provided at your cost, not retail. There are no per-creator cash fees on standard visits.

What if a creator does not post?

The goal is for creators to post within 48 hours of the visit, and on-time posts earn them Momentum. If no post has appeared within a week of the visit, you get a replacement visit and the slot does not count against your cap. Creators who no-show or do not post lose standing, and repeat offenders are removed from the roster entirely.

What is the Visit Guarantee?

If a booked creator does not show up or does not post, that visit does not count against your monthly visits. It is replaced.

What is the refund policy?

Paid bookings are held by Magpie until the post is live. If the visit does not happen or the post never lands, the business is refunded in full automatically. If we cannot deliver the service, including a founding market that does not open, founding businesses receive a full refund of what they paid for that market. Plans run month to month after the 3 month minimum. Cancelling stops future billing. Visits already delivered are not refundable.
