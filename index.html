How Magpie works

Three steps. No agency, no guesswork.

1
Shop your city

Browse local creators like products, each with one number up front: the share of their followers who live in your city. Save the ones you like, compare them side by side.

2
Book the visit

Offer a comped visit — a meal, a cut, a tasting — or pay their listed rate. They come in like a regular, enjoy it for real, and post their honest take within 48 hours.

3
Count who came in

Every post carries the creator's code, so you see exactly how many visits it brought. Good creators get booked again. That's the whole loop.

Why local wins

Built different, on purpose.

not by famous.

Our story

Named for the bird that never leaves.

Why we started

The places that make a neighborhood worth living in run on word of mouth. But the creator economy was built for big brands with big budgets, and the neighborhood got left out. Meanwhile, every neighborhood already has people whose word their neighbors actually trust. Magpie connects the two: local creators, local businesses, one neighborhood at a time.

Why a magpie

Magpies are social and vocal. They gather the bright, shiny things in their neighborhood and bring them home. And they stay in the same area their whole lives: local for life. That is the whole idea behind Magpie: find the bright things where you live, and bring people to them.

Founded by Jeff Rimmer, who has spent 2 decades in local media and startups, and most recently led marketing for large brands and Fortune 100 companies, including influencer marketing at scale.
