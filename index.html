How SIGNL works

Three steps. No agency, no guesswork.

1
Shop your city

Browse local creators like products, each with one number up front: the share of their followers who live in your city. Save the ones you like, compare them side by side.

2
Book the visit

Offer a comped visit — a meal, a cut, a tasting — or pay their listed rate. They come in like a regular, enjoy it for real, and post their honest take within 48 hours.

3
Count who came in

Every post carries the creator's code, so you see exactly how many visits it brought. Good creators get booked again. That's the whole loop.

Why local wins

Built different, on purpose.

Ranked by local,
not by famous.

Every other platform sorts by follower count. SIGNL sorts by the share of a creator's audience that lives in your city — the only number that fills your seats.

Counted,
not guessed.

Each post carries the creator's code. When a follower walks in and uses it, the visit is counted. You stop wondering if it worked and start knowing.

Paid when posted,
not net-90.

Creators set their own rate and keep 97% of it. The money is held before the visit and released the moment the post is live. Nobody chases an invoice.
