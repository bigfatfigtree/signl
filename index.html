Two ways it pays

Comped, or paid. The business picks the lane.

Lane one

The comp

A business comps the creator a meal, a service, or an experience. The creator visits, posts honestly within 48 hours, and the business can share that post natively. Simple, and it is how local favorites have always spread.

Lane two

The paid booking

The creator lists their own rate, starting at $50. The business pays the rate plus 3%, the creator keeps 97%, and SIGNL holds the payment until the post is live. A paid visit also lets the business repost the post and run it as an ad for 6 months. Paid bookings depend on the business, and they are never guaranteed.

Business plans: $299 / $599 / $999 a month, about $37 per creator visit on Self-Service. Founding businesses join the list now and activate when their market opens.
