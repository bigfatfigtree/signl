The comp rules, read these

Simple deal, enforced fairly

The comp is the pay

A free meal or service is your compensation. Creators are never on a payroll, businesses supply the comp, you supply the post.

48-hour posting standard

The 48-hour goal: your post should be live within 48 hours of your visit, and on-time posts earn Momentum. A post inside a week still counts. Missing the week, or going silent, is what costs roster standing.

FTC disclosure is required

Every post must clearly disclose the comp (e.g. #gifted / "meal provided by…"). No exceptions, it protects you and the business.

No-shows lose their roster spot

Claim it, show up. A no-show without notice, or a visit with no post, removes you from the roster. Businesses plan around your visit.

Honest posts only

Nobody can require a positive review. Post your real take. Businesses that pressure creators get removed, your credibility is the whole product.

Creator FAQ

Questions, answered straight

Does it cost anything to join Magpie as a creator?

No. Joining and staying on the roster is free, and creators are never charged fees. Your compensation on standard visits is the comp itself, the meal, service or experience the business provides.

How do creators get paid on Magpie?

Two ways. On a comped visit, the pay is the comp itself: a free meal, service or experience in exchange for an honest post. On a paid booking, any creator at any tier can be booked at the rate they set, and they keep 97 percent.

Do I have to post something positive?

Never. Honest posts only, that is the entire product. Businesses cannot require a positive post or review, and a business that pressures a creator is removed from the platform. Post your real experience, in your own voice.

What do I have to post, and how fast?

1 post or reel plus 1 story that shares the post about the visit, with the goal of posting within 48 hours of the visit, and a week as the outside limit, with a clear disclosure that it was comped (for example “comped” or #gifted), as FTC guidelines require. If a claimed visit gets no post inside the week, the business gets a replacement visit and the creator's roster standing takes the hit; repeat no-shows lose their spot.

How many followers do I need to join Magpie?

Magpie starts at 500 total followers across your platforms. Your tier (Neighbors 500–2,000, Locals 2,000–15,000, Headliners 15,000+) is set by that stacked total and rises as you grow. A small, genuinely local audience beats a big, scattered one.

I'm not in Hampton Roads. Can I still join Magpie?

Yes. Magpie launches city by city, and Hampton Roads, Virginia is where Magpie began, one of 45 founding markets in 13 countries. Apply with your home city and you are first on its list: when your city opens, its early applicants become its founding roster. Cities are picked by signup counts.

Are there brand deals for creators in Virginia Beach and Hampton Roads?

The Virginia Beach founding roster covers all of Hampton Roads: Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Chesapeake, Portsmouth, Suffolk, Hampton, Newport News and Williamsburg.
