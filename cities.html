How a city gets picked

/ 01

Sign up, name your city

Creator applications, business applications and city votes all count, every one of them carries a city. That count is the ranking.

/ 02

The board is the ranking

When a city's list gets deep enough to launch a real roster, it opens. Loud cities launch; quiet cities wait. It's that simple, and it's public.

Your list founds your city

Founding markets

Thirty-seven founding markets across eight countries

Founding sign-ups are open in every market below. Markets open in waves over the launch window as each founding roster completes, founding businesses hold founding pricing, and every market carries the same founding status. Seven markets are defined by their full city lists: those lists are SIGNL's published metro definitions, the geographies a creator's local audience percentage is measured against. The Virginia Beach market covers all of Hampton Roads. The other markets are listed by their anchor city, with their full definitions published as each market opens. Do not see your city? Add your city at the top of this page: anyone can sign up from any city, listed or not, and an unlisted signup starts your city's list.
