How creator visits work

On standard visits, a creator's compensation is the comped meal, service, or experience offered by the business — not cash. In exchange, the creator posts about the visit within 48 hours. Posts must be honest, and every post must carry a clear disclosure (for example, "comped" or #ad), as required by FTC guidelines. Creators who no-show or fail to post lose standing and may be removed from the roster.

SIGNL guarantees tracked deliverables — posts within 48 hours of completed visits, verified in your dashboard — not a specific number of creators, which no honest platform can promise. Businesses set the value of their own comp offers. Businesses may not require positive posts or reviews; honest posts are the product.
