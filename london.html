For creators: the deal, up front

SIGNL is free for creators. Always. Businesses pay the platform. You never pay to join, to list, or to get booked.

Comp visits are the core. Paid bookings sit on top: set your own rate, the business pays it, and the platform fee is 6 percent total, split evenly, so you keep 97 percent of your rate. Paid bookings are held by SIGNL until your post is live. Headliners (15,000+ followers) are also first in line for paid hero campaigns when businesses run them.

Why London, why now

London runs on its neighborhoods

A city of villages

London is a patchwork of villages that never quite merged: Shoreditch eats differently from Brixton, and Notting Hill differently again. Local businesses need creators whose followers actually live in their patch, and this roster is built that way.

Markets set the table

Borough Market mornings, Camden stalls, street food markets in every borough: London's food conversation starts at markets and moves to tables. SIGNL creators post both, and locals trust what they see there.

The curry house debate

Brick Lane legends, Brixton institutions, who does it right and who trades on name alone: London argues its curry houses like its football clubs. Creators with real opinions are the ones who fill dining rooms.

New rooms open weekly

Railway arches in Peckham, rooftops in Shoreditch, gastropubs reinvented across Hackney: the map keeps changing, and every opening needs locals talking about it. Founding creators get first pick of each one.

For London businesses

London businesses: be the room locals post about

Londoners choose the neighborhood spot over the chain on the high street, and they choose it because someone they follow ate there. Founding businesses get that someone: real local creators, comped at your cost, posting honestly to the followers who fill your tables.

Ads reach everyone except the people who live nearby. SIGNL puts London creators who live here inside your business, posting to neighbors who can walk in this week.

/ 01

Join the founding business list

Apply in a few minutes. Your category spot in your trade area is held in application order, and nothing is charged today.

/ 02

Founding pricing, held for you

$299 a month for Self-Service (up to 8 creator visits a month, about $37 per creator visit), $599 a month for Growth (up to 20 creator visits a month, about $30 per creator visit) or $999 a month for Managed (up to 30 creator visits a month), with a 3-month minimum and then month to month. The only other cost is the comp the business chooses to offer, provided at its cost, not retail. Founding businesses keep founding pricing while they remain subscribed, and the plan begins when the London market opens.

/ 03

Activate when London opens

Pick creators from the founding roster, offer a comp at your cost, and get honest, tracked posts delivered in 48 hours* of each visit.

*Creators have up to one week to post. 48 hours is the standard they are held to, and hitting it earns their Momentum. If a week passes with no post, the visit is replaced and does not count against your plan.

The Visit Guarantee

If a booked creator does not show up or does not post, that visit does not count against your monthly visits. It is replaced.

How content rights work: on a comp visit, you can share the creator's post natively with the creator credited, and nothing more: no downloads, no reposting the file, no ads. On a paid visit, you can also repost the content on your own channels and run it as an ad for 6 months from the post date. The creator always owns the content. Rights follow the visit, never the plan.
