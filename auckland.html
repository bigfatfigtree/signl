For creators: the deal, up front

Magpie is free for creators. Always. Businesses pay the platform. You never pay to join, to list, or to get booked.

Comp visits are the core. Paid bookings sit on top: set your own rate from a NZ$90 floor, the business pays it, and the platform fee is 6 percent total, split evenly, so you keep 97 percent of your rate. Paid bookings are held by Magpie until your post is live. Headliners (15,000+ followers) are also first in line for featured paid campaigns when businesses run them.

Why Auckland, why now

Auckland eats by suburb

A supercity of villages

Auckland merged dozens of towns into one city, but Ponsonby, Grey Lynn, Devonport and Howick each kept their own high street, their own cafés and their own regulars. Local businesses need the people who post each of them.

Two harbours, two appetites

Waitematā suburbs eat by the water in Mission Bay and Takapuna; the Manukau side runs on markets and family tables from Manukau to Pukekohe. Magpie creators post both cities inside the city.

The flat white standard is brutal

Which café pours the best flat white, which bakery sells out by nine, which Dominion Road dumpling spot is worth the bus ride: Auckland argues its food warmly and endlessly. Creators with real opinions are the ones who move a dining room.

Shore to south

From Takapuna and Albany on the Shore to Papakura, Pukekohe and the western beaches, small rooms keep opening, and each one survives on local word of mouth. Founding creators get first pick of every new table.

For Auckland businesses

Auckland businesses: be the room locals post about

Aucklanders choose the neighbourhood café over the famous name, and they choose it because someone they follow ate there. Founding businesses get that someone: real local creators, comped at your cost, posting honestly to the followers who fill your tables.

Ads reach everyone except the people who live nearby. Magpie puts Auckland creators who live here inside your business, posting to neighbors who can walk in this week.

/ 01

Join the founding business list

Apply in a few minutes. Your category spot in your trade area is held in application order, and nothing is charged today.

/ 02

Founding pricing, held for you

NZ$349 a month for Self-Service (up to 5 creator visits a month, about NZ$70 per creator visit), NZ$619 a month for Growth (up to 10 creator visits a month, about NZ$62 per creator visit) or NZ$1,239 a month for Managed (up to 20 creator visits a month), with a 3-month minimum and then month to month. Founding businesses get NZ$175 off the first month, and all prices are plus GST. Visits you don't use roll into the next month, then expire. The only other cost is the comp the business chooses to offer, provided at its cost, not retail. Founding businesses keep founding pricing while they remain subscribed, and the plan begins when the Auckland market opens.

/ 03

Activate when Auckland opens

Pick creators from the founding roster, offer a comp at your cost, and get honest, tracked posts delivered in 48 hours* of each visit.

*Creators have up to one week to post. The goal is to post within 48 hours of the visit, and hitting it earns their Momentum. If a week passes with no post, the visit is replaced and does not count against your plan.

The Visit Guarantee

If a booked creator does not show up or does not post, that visit does not count against your monthly visits. It is replaced.

How content rights work: on a comp visit, you can share the creator's post natively with the creator credited, and nothing more: no downloads, no reposting the file, no ads. On a paid visit, you can also repost the content on your own channels and run it as an ad for 90 days under the standard license included in the booking. The creator always owns the content. Rights follow the visit, never the plan.
