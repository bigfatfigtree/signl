Honest FAQ

Straight answers, no sales talk

What does a fitness business comp?

A class pack, a guest pass, a trial week, a personal training session. Whatever you would offer a prospect, offered to a local creator whose followers live close enough to join this month.

Will this fill my slow classes?

That is the standard play: aim comp offers at off-peak classes and new-program launches. Managed coordinators build the calendar around your schedule, and every visit is tracked like any other.

Is SIGNL only for boutique studios?

No. Any fitness business in the list below fits, from climbing gyms and CrossFit boxes to dance studios and indoor golf. If members join in person, local creators can fill the room.

How much does it cost?

Published pricing: $299 a month for Self-Service (up to 8 creator visits a month, about $37 per creator visit), $599 a month for Growth (up to 20 creator visits a month, about $30 per creator visit) or $999 a month for Managed (up to 30 creator visits a month), with a 3-month minimum, then month to month. Your only other cost is the comp you choose to offer, provided at your cost, not retail. There are no per-creator cash fees on standard visits.

What if a creator does not post?

The 48-hour rule is the backbone of the platform. If a post does not appear within 48 hours of a visit, you get a replacement visit and the slot does not count against your cap. Creators who no-show or do not post lose standing, and repeat offenders are removed from the roster entirely.

Do creators have to post something positive?

Never. Creators post their honest experience, and no business on SIGNL may require a positive post or review. That honesty is exactly what makes the posts believable to the people reading them. Businesses that deliver a good experience get good posts.
