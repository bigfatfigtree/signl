The creators our neighbors actually follow.

Magpie's editors picked 40 creators across Raleigh and the Triangle who are worth a local business's attention: people who live here, post for their neighbors, and show up week after week. How we picked them

Rooted here beats big here

Three things to know before you read the list.

/ 01

Rooted here

Every creator on this list lives their beat here: their neighborhoods, their restaurants, their teams, their weekends. The content is made in Raleigh, for Raleigh.

/ 02

Local audience over follower count

What a local business is really reaching is nearby people who can walk in. A smaller creator whose crowd is here can be worth more than a giant whose crowd is everywhere else, so follower count never decided who made this list. The list below is grouped by what they do, not ranked by size: a creator with 900 neighbors who all live here matters more on this list than a million followers somewhere else.

/ 03

Checked by hand

Every name was verified from public profiles: a real person, based here in Raleigh, posting recently and consistently. If a creator goes quiet or moves away, they come off the list.
