Creator FAQ

The deep FAQ

Does it cost anything to join SIGNL as a creator?

No. Joining and staying on the roster is free. Your compensation on standard visits is the comp itself: the meal, service or experience the business provides. The only cut SIGNL ever takes is 3 percent of a paid rate you set yourself, if a business books you at it. You keep the other 97 percent.

How do creators get paid on SIGNL?

On standard visits, the comp is the pay: a free meal, service or experience in exchange for an honest post. Creators in the top tier (15,000+ followers) are also eligible for paid hero campaigns when businesses run them, paid in cash on top of comps. Any creator, at any tier, can also set a paid rate on their profile. When a business books you directly at your rate, you keep 97 percent of it, because SIGNL takes only 3 percent of the creator's rate.

What are the disclosure rules?

Every post about a comped visit must clearly disclose that the visit was comped, as FTC guidelines require: say it in the caption or on screen, and a tag like #gifted is the common shorthand. Disclosure is not optional and it is not fine print; it is what makes your post trustworthy to your followers and safe for you.

What is the 48-hour rule, exactly?

From the moment your visit happens, your post about it must be live within 48 hours: a story, post or reel on your own account, with disclosure. The clock is the backbone of the platform, because a business comping a visit on Friday needs the post while the weekend is still being planned, not next month.

What happens if I miss the 48 hours, or do not post?

The business gets a replacement visit that does not count against its plan, and your roster standing takes the hit. Repeat no-shows are removed from the roster. If something real goes wrong, message us before the window closes; standing is about reliability, and reliability includes communicating.

Do I have to post something positive?

Never. Honest posts only, in your own voice. A business cannot require a positive post or review, cannot ask you to soften an honest one, and cannot retaliate when a post is fair and true. A business that pressures a creator is removed from the platform.

Can a business ask me to delete or change my post?

A business can flag a post it believes is factually false, and SIGNL reviews those with both sides. It cannot ask you to change an honest opinion, and honest opinions are never removed for being unflattering.

How do tiers work, and can I move up?

Your tier is set by follower count: Neighbors (500 to 2,000), Locals (2,000 to 15,000) and Creators (15,000+). You self-select your band honestly when you sign up, and your tier moves up as your following grows. Every tier gets comps; the Creators band adds eligibility for paid cash campaigns and first pick of grand openings and launches.

I have fewer than 500 followers. Can I still apply?

Yes, anyone can apply at any follower count. Your tier is set by your follower count and rises as you grow. A small, genuinely local audience beats a big, scattered one, because businesses on SIGNL shop by audience location, not raw count.

What is my local audience percentage, and who sees it?

It is the share of your followers who live in your SIGNL market, and it is the number businesses weigh most when choosing creators. Profiles label where the figure comes from: verified from your own Instagram audience data when you connect it, or self-reported until then. A 1,400-person audience that is 70 percent local beats a 200,000-person audience scattered across a country, and SIGNL is built to prove it.

How do referrals and Momentum points work?

Your profile carries a personal invite link. Every creator who joins through it is credited to your roster. Momentum points reward building that roster: +3 when a creator joins through your link, and +10 when they are approved. Roster standing is part of the order businesses see when they choose creators, so the people who build the roster get seen first.

My city is not in the founding markets. Can I still join?

Yes. Apply in the app with your real home city. If your city is not one of the 39 founding markets, your sign-up starts its list: cities are picked by signup counts, and when your city opens, its early applicants become its founding roster, in order.

Is SIGNL a UGC creator program?

Partly, with a difference. In a typical UGC program you produce content and hand it to a brand to post on its own channels. On SIGNL you post to your own audience as a local creator, which is why local businesses value it: your followers trust you. Delivered posts are also saved to the business's content library with reuse rights, so your work keeps working after the visit.
