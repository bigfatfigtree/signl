For creators: the deal, up front

SIGNL is free for creators. Always. Businesses pay the platform. You never pay to join, to list, or to get booked.

Comp visits are the core. Paid bookings sit on top: set your own rate, the business pays it, and the platform fee is 6 percent total, split evenly, so you keep 97 percent of your rate. Paid bookings are held by SIGNL until your post is live. Headliners (15,000+ followers) are also first in line for paid hero campaigns when businesses run them.

Why Cincinnati should open

A river city with strong rulings

The chili constitution

Three-way, four-way, five-way, cheese coneys at midnight: Cincinnati chili parlors are civic institutions, and the order you grew up with is the correct one. Institutions with loyalists are SIGNL fuel.

Over-the-Rhine, rebuilt by locals

One of America's great historic districts came back through independent restaurants, breweries and shops, block by block. That comeback is the exact energy a founding roster amplifies.

Two states, one night out

The river is a bridge, not a border: Covington and Newport across the water are part of the same evening. Creators here post a two-state metro without thinking about it.

Northside does its own thing

Cincinnati's proudly independent streak runs strongest in Northside: record stores, taverns, kitchens with cult followings. Cult followings are the point of the whole platform.

Creator tiers

Where do you land?

Tiers are set automatically by your total audience across platforms, and they rise as you grow. A small, genuinely local audience beats a big, scattered one.

Neighbors
500 – 2k followers
  • Everyday people with a local following
  • Comp only
  • Perfect first step into local posting
Locals
2k – 15k followers
  • Regular local posters, the roster's sweet spot
  • Comp plus roster perks
  • Priority access to new businesses
Headliners
15k+ followers
  • Established local creators
  • Comp plus eligibility for paid hero campaigns
  • First pick of grand openings and launches

Under 500 followers, or just starting out? Apply anyway. Your tier is set by your follower count, and it moves up as you grow. What matters most is not the number, it's that your audience is actually local and actually engages. Pick your band honestly when you sign up.

Cincinnati FAQ

Questions, answered straight

Is SIGNL open in Cincinnati yet?

No, and we will not pretend otherwise. Cincinnati is not one of SIGNL's 39 founding markets yet, so there are no offers and no roster there today. Sign-ups from Cincinnati are counted city by city, and the cities with the most sign-ups open first. Sign up now and you are first on the list when Cincinnati opens.

How do I sign up if Cincinnati is not listed?

In the SIGNL app, choose My city is not listed yet and type Cincinnati. That is a real sign-up, counted toward opening Cincinnati, and it puts you at the front of its founding list if the city opens. It takes about two minutes.

Does it cost anything to join SIGNL as a creator?

No. SIGNL is free for creators. Always. Businesses pay the platform. You never pay to join, to list, or to get booked. Your compensation on standard visits is the comp itself: the meal, service or experience the business provides.

What would I actually post about in Cincinnati?

A cheese coney ruling at your parlor, an OTR patio night, a Northside record-store day, a riverfront evening that ends in Covington. The Standard Visit is 1 post or reel plus 2 stories, live within 48 hours of the visit, honest and clearly disclosed as comped.

I am a student at UC or Xavier. Can I join?

Yes. Name your school when you sign up or finish your profile and your profile carries your campus, so businesses near your school can find you first. The Standard Visit fits between classes, and alcohol offers are 21+ only.
