For creators: the deal, up front

Magpie is free for creators. Always. Businesses pay the platform. You never pay to join, to list, or to get booked.

Comp visits are the core. Paid bookings sit on top: set your own rate, the business pays it, and the platform fee is 6 percent total, split evenly, so you keep 97 percent of your rate. Paid bookings are held by Magpie until your post is live. Headliners (15,000+ followers) are also first in line for featured paid campaigns when businesses run them.

Why Nashville, why now

Music City runs on regulars

Music City is a working town

Nashville's real music economy is songwriters, session players and rooms where the next name plays to twenty regulars. That is exactly the audience local businesses need: people who actually go out here, every week.

Neighborhoods over Broadway

Visitors crowd Lower Broad. Locals eat in East Nashville, gather in The Nations and celebrate in 12 South. Magpie creators post that second city, the one residents actually trust for where to go.

Hot chicken is a debate here

Heat levels, white bread, pickles, who does it right and who is pretending: Nashville argues its food like its football. Creators with real opinions are the ones who move a dining room.

New rooms open constantly

Wedgewood-Houston galleries, Gulch rooftops, Sylvan Park cafes: the map keeps changing, and every opening needs locals talking about it. Founding creators get first pick of each one.

The number that matters

Local reach beats follower count here

Nashville is a visitor town, and follower counts cannot tell a neighbor from a bachelorette party that flew home on Sunday. Magpie can. Every creator here is ranked by local reach: the share of their audience that actually lives in the Nashville metro.

That is the headline number on every Nashville profile, ahead of follower count. A Germantown regular with 4,000 followers, most of them Nashvillians, is worth more to a Germantown restaurant than a 90,000-follower account whose audience is scattered across the country. Businesses browsing the roster see the local share first, so the creators who get invited are the ones whose followers can actually walk in.

It cuts both ways, and both lanes are open from the first breath: take a comped visit, a meal, service or experience for the standard post and story, or be booked at your own listed rate and keep 97 percent. Local reach is what gets you found for either one. New to the idea? Read what local reach is, then the Nashville creator playbook: how Nashville creators get booked by local businesses.

The comp rules, read these

Simple deal, enforced fairly

The comp is the pay

A free meal or service is your compensation. Creators are never on a payroll, businesses supply the comp, you supply the post.

48-hour posting standard

The 48-hour goal: your post should be live within 48 hours of your visit, and on-time posts earn Momentum. A post inside a week still counts. Missing the week, or going silent, is what costs roster standing.

FTC disclosure is required

Every post must clearly disclose the comp (e.g. #gifted / "meal provided by…"). No exceptions, it protects you and the business.

No-shows lose their roster spot

Claim it, show up. A no-show without notice, or a visit with no post, removes you from the roster. Businesses plan around your visit.

Honest posts only

Nobody can require a positive review. Post your real take. Businesses that pressure creators get removed, your credibility is the whole product.

Founding pricing, held for you

$199 a month for Self-Service (up to 5 creator visits a month, about $40 per creator visit), $349 a month for Growth (up to 10 creator visits a month, about $35 per creator visit) or $699 a month for Managed (up to 20 creator visits a month), with a 3-month minimum and then month to month. / 03

Activate when Nashville opens

Pick creators from the founding roster, offer a comp at your cost, and get honest, tracked posts delivered in 48 hours* of each visit.

*Creators have up to one week to post. The goal is to post within 48 hours of the visit, and hitting it earns their Momentum. If a week passes with no post, the visit is replaced and does not count against your plan.

The Visit Guarantee

If a booked creator does not show up or does not post, that visit does not count against your monthly visits. It is replaced.

How content rights work: on a comp visit, you can share the creator's post natively with the creator credited, and nothing more: no downloads, no reposting the file, no ads. On a paid visit, you can also repost the content on your own channels and run it as an ad for 90 days under the standard license included in the booking. The creator always owns the content. Rights follow the visit, never the plan.
