Straight answers

Questions, answered straight

What is the Nashville Creators to Watch list?

It is SIGNL's own editorial list of Nashville creators we think local businesses will want to book first, across food and drink, style and beauty, fitness and wellness, home and living, music and nightlife, and family and everyday life. SIGNL picked every name on it from public creator profiles.

What does claiming my page mean?

Claiming your page means joining SIGNL as a founding creator. That is the whole meaning, and nothing is hidden: the claim button takes you to SIGNL sign-up with your handle and Nashville already filled in. Joining is free for creators, always. Once you claim, you set your rates and the page is yours to control.

What shows on my listing before I claim it?

Only your public handle and your category. No photo, no bio, no rates. Those appear only when you claim your page and add them yourself.

What do I get when I claim?

A live SIGNL page with your rates on it, ready to share, and a founding creator spot on the Nashville roster. When Nashville businesses start hiring local creators on SIGNL, they book from that roster, and founding creators are on it from day one.

Is SIGNL live in Nashville yet?

Nashville is a founding market: founding creators are joining now, and businesses sign up when we are ready to launch. Claiming now puts you on the founding roster before the market opens.

Can I be taken off the list?

Yes. Email hello@joinsignl.com and we will remove your listing. Claiming is how you control the page; removal takes you off the list entirely. Either way, it is your call.

Does it cost anything to be on the list or to claim?

No. SIGNL is free for creators. Always. Businesses pay the platform. You never pay to join, to list, or to get booked.
