How we picked them

Rooted here beats big here

Three things to know before you read the list.

/ 01

Rooted in the metro

Every creator on this list lives their beat here: their neighborhoods, their restaurants, their teams, their weekends. The content is made in Dallas–Fort Worth, for Dallas–Fort Worth.

/ 02

Local audience over follower count

What a local business is really reaching is nearby people who can walk in. A smaller creator whose crowd is here can be worth more than a giant whose crowd is everywhere else, so follower count never decided who made this list.

/ 03

Checked by hand

Every name was verified from public profiles: a real person, based in the metro, posting recently and consistently. If a creator goes quiet or moves away, they come off the list.
