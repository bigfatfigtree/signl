Honest FAQ

Straight answers, no sales talk

Can creators make treatment claims about my practice?

No. Posts about health and aesthetic services on SIGNL are personal experiences only: what the visit was like, never what a treatment does, and never a promised result. Claims are not allowed on the platform, and every post carries full disclosure that the visit was comped.

Who is responsible for advertising compliance?

Your practice stays responsible for its own advertising rules, the same as any other channel you use. SIGNL's rules (personal experiences only, no treatment claims, full disclosure, no required positive posts) are designed to keep creator posts inside the lines a practice can stand behind.

What services fit a comp?

Whatever your practice is comfortable offering a guest: a consultation, an eligible service or a visit experience, set by you on every offer. Creators opt in knowing exactly what the visit includes, and they post about their own experience of it.

Can I require a good review in exchange?

No, and this matters more in healthcare than anywhere else. No business on SIGNL may require a positive post or review, on the platform or anywhere else. Practices that deliver a good experience earn good word of mouth honestly.

How much does it cost?

Published pricing: $299 a month for Self-Service (up to 8 creator visits a month, about $37 per creator visit), $599 a month for Growth (up to 20 creator visits a month, about $30 per creator visit) or $999 a month for Managed (up to 30 creator visits a month), with a 3-month minimum, then month to month. Your only other cost is the comp you choose to offer, provided at your cost, not retail. There are no per-creator cash fees on standard visits.

What if a creator does not post?

The 48-hour rule is the backbone of the platform. If a post does not appear within 48 hours of a visit, you get a replacement visit and the slot does not count against your cap. Creators who no-show or do not post lose standing, and repeat offenders are removed from the roster entirely.
