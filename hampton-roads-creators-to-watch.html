How this guide works

An editorial list, kept honest

Three things are true about every name below, and they stay true whether the creator ever touches Magpie or not.

/ 01

Picked from public profiles

Every creator here earned the spot with a local audience, a clear niche and consistent posting. Nobody pays to be listed, and nobody can buy a spot.

/ 02

Listed free, no strings

Being listed does not sign anyone up for anything. Each listing is complete as it stands: a line on what the creator is known for, their category, and a link to claim their Magpie page.

/ 03

Rates are the creator's choice

A listed creator who joins Magpie can add their rates to their listing and take bookings through the platform. That step is optional, and it is always the creator's call.

Using the guide

Two ways to use this guide

/ Businesses

Find your next creator here

Creators who have joined Magpie can be booked through the platform at the rates they set, with the Visit Guarantee behind the booking. Start with the founding business page.

/ Creators

On the list? It is yours either way

Your listing needs nothing from you. If you would like businesses to be able to book you through this guide, the link under your listing adds your rates: that means joining Magpie as a founding creator, free, and your listing here stays exactly as it is either way.

/ Not listed

The guide is a start, not a gate

Any Hampton Roads creator with 500 or more total followers across their platforms can join Magpie as a founding creator right now, listed or not. The roster is wider than any one guide. Join the founding roster.

Straight answers

Questions, answered straight

What is the Hampton Roads Creators to Watch guide?

It is Magpie's editorial guide to Hampton Roads creators worth following and working with, across food and drink, style and beauty, fitness and wellness, home and real estate, music and nightlife, and family and everyday life. We publish it so local businesses and readers can find Hampton Roads creators in one place.

How were creators picked?

From public profiles: a local Hampton Roads audience, a clear niche and consistent posting. The picks are editorial, made by Magpie. Nobody pays to be listed, and nobody has to join anything to be listed.

Does being listed mean I joined Magpie?

No. The guide is editorial, and a listing is not a membership. Joining Magpie is a separate, optional step a listed creator can take if they want to add their rates and take bookings through the platform.

What does claiming my Magpie page mean?

It means joining Magpie as a founding creator, free, always. You set your rates, your Magpie page goes live, and businesses can book you through Magpie when the Hampton Roads market opens. Your listing in this guide stays exactly the same whether you claim or not.

I run a business. How do I work with a creator in this guide?

Creators who have joined Magpie can be booked through the platform at the rates they set. Hampton Roads businesses sign up when we are ready to launch; the founding business page explains the program in the meantime.

Can my listing be changed or removed?

Yes. Email hello@magpielocal.com and we will update or remove your listing. It is your name and your work; the listing should be right.

Does it cost anything to be listed?

No. Listing is editorial and free. Joining Magpie is also free for creators, always. Businesses pay the platform.
