Honest FAQ

Straight answers, no sales talk

What can an experience business comp?

A ticket, a seat, a rental, a tour, a class, a day pass, a night's stay. If it can be booked, it can be comped, and a creator living it on camera is the most believable listing it will ever have.

We are seasonal. Does a subscription still make sense?

Plans carry a 3-month minimum and then run month to month, so a seasonal business can run SIGNL through its season. Events and launches can also use the one-time Grand Opening Burst ($1,500) for a concentrated wave of visits.

Can hotels and rentals use SIGNL?

Yes. Boutique hotels, inns and short-term rental hosts comp stays, day passes and on-site experiences, and creators post to followers planning trips and staycations.

How much does it cost?

Published pricing: $299 a month for Self-Service (up to 8 creator visits a month, about $37 per creator visit) or $799 a month for Managed, with a 3-month minimum, then month to month. Your only other cost is the comp you choose to offer. There are no per-creator cash fees on standard visits.

What if a creator does not post?

The 48-hour rule is the backbone of the platform. If a post does not appear within 48 hours of a visit, you get a replacement visit and the slot does not count against your cap. Creators who no-show or do not post lose standing, and repeat offenders are removed from the roster entirely.

Do creators have to post something positive?

Never. Creators post their honest experience, and no business on SIGNL may require a positive post or review. That honesty is exactly what makes the posts believable to the people reading them. Businesses that deliver a good experience get good posts.
