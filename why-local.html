The problem

Lost in the sea of national

National influencer platforms are impressive machines pointed at the wrong target for a local business. They optimize for reach, and reach is the one thing you cannot spend at a register.

/ 01

Reach is the wrong unit

National platforms rank creators by total followers and sell impressions by the million. Impressions in forty states do not put one customer at your door. Reach you cannot sell to is trivia, no matter how big the number printed on it.

/ 02

Your market is ten miles wide

Nearly every customer a local business will ever get lives within about ten miles of the door. That circle is the whole market. Marketing that lands outside it may look busy in a report, but it never turns into a Tuesday night crowd.

/ 03

Concentration is what converts

When posts keep landing inside one community, the same neighbors see your name again and again, from people they actually trust. That repetition is what turns a post into a habit, and a habit into a regular.

What local buys you

Concentration is the whole strategy

/ 01

Regulars, not impressions

An impression is a stranger you will never meet, counted once and gone. A regular is revenue that repeats every week. Local audiences are the only kind that can become regulars, because they are the only kind who can come back.

/ 02

Repeat visibility in one place

One community sees your business again and again, from different trusted voices: the taco post on Monday, the salon chair on Friday. Familiarity compounds inside a neighborhood in a way a national blast never can.

/ 03

Creators who can walk in weekly

Your roster lives here. They can come back for the new menu, the seasonal special, the grand reopening, because it is their neighborhood too. A national creator visits once, if the shipping label reaches them.

The evidence

Why local beats reach

/ 01

People trust people like them

92% of consumers trust recommendations from people they know over any form of advertising (Nielsen). A local creator's post reads like a recommendation, because it is one: a neighbor, in a real spot, in your feed.

/ 02

Followers who can actually show up

Local followers can act the same day they see the post. Magpie creator profiles show what share of a creator's audience is actually in your market, labeled by source, so businesses choose reach that can walk through the door, not a vanity count.

/ 03

Honest posts convert

Creators post their real experience, never a script, and businesses can never require a positive post. That is exactly why the posts get believed, saved and shared. On Magpie, businesses never buy praise. They buy presence.
