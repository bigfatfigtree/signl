The counting rules

How the counting works

/ 01

Real records only

Counts come from SIGNL signup records, deduplicated on handle and email, with test records excluded. Nothing is rounded up and nothing is padded: if the count is 3, the board says 3.

/ 02

Posted Mondays

The boards are pulled and posted once a week, on Mondays, and the date on this page says when the current counts were pulled. If a week is ever missed, the date says that honestly too.

/ 03

Places, not people

No creator name, handle or photo ever appears on a board. A creator shows up in a SIGNL story or feature only with their own permission, and that permission is always separate from signing up.

Race FAQ

Fair questions

How are the counts gathered?

From real SIGNL signup records, pulled once a week and posted on Mondays. Signups are deduplicated on handle and email, and test records are excluded. The roster board counts approved founding creators. Nothing is rounded up: if the count is 3, the board says 3.

What does the leading city get?

When SIGNL opens its next wave of founding markets, the Next Wave Race leader is first in line, and the winning count is announced here. Opening still depends on roster depth and founding businesses in that market, so the board promises order, not a date. The Campus Race leader each month gets the next campus activation push.

Why are there no creator names or handles on the boards?

The boards rank places, not people. Creators appear in SIGNL stories and features only with their own permission, and that permission is separate from signing up. A board entry is a place and a number, so anyone can check the count and nobody is marketed without saying yes.
