Two ways it pays

Get comped, or get paid at your own rate

Every Magpie visit runs one of two lanes, and the lane is set by the business's offer. Here is both of them, in one breath, with nothing fine-printed. For the comp lane in depth, see how getting comped works; for the small-following case, see brand deals with 500 followers.

/ Lane one

Get comped

A business covers a meal, a service or an experience. You visit like a real customer and post honestly about it. On these visits, the comp itself is the pay.

/ Lane two

Get paid

You list your own rate on your profile, starting at $50. When a business books you at your rate, Magpie holds the payment and releases it when your post is live, and you keep 97 percent. Paid bookings depend on the business, so they are never guaranteed, but the rate is always yours.

/ The visit

One post, one story sharing the post, 48 hours

Whichever lane, the Standard Visit is the same: 1 post or reel plus 1 story that shares the post, with the goal of posting within 48 hours of the visit, with up to a week at the outside, honest and clearly disclosed. You always own your content, and no business can require a positive post.

Your content stays yours. Rights follow the visit, never a plan. On a comped visit, the business can share your post natively, with you credited, and nothing more: no downloads, no reposts, no ads. On a paid visit, the business can also repost the post and run it as an ad for 90 days under the standard license included in the booking. Either way, the content is yours and it lives on your profile, building your record.

Can I get brand deals in Austin with 500 followers?

Yes. Brand deals in Austin start at 500 followers in total on Magpie, and your counts stack across Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, X and LinkedIn. How much do Austin creators get paid on Magpie?

It depends on the lane, and you control one of them. On a comped visit, the meal, service or experience itself is your compensation. On a paid booking, you list your own rate on your profile, starting at $50, and you keep 97 percent of it when a business books you at that rate.
