Restaurants, automated · vs SIGNL

Mustard vs SIGNL

Mustard is an automated platform built for restaurants: the restaurant sets a food credit and campaign details, and the software invites creators, generates briefs, tracks posts and reports results in a live dashboard. Its published base plan is fully automated, with no dedicated human support.

MustardSIGNL
The visits
The published plan includes 3 influencer matches and hosted visits per month, and the restaurant supplies the food credit.
Every SIGNL plan is metered in creator visits: up to 8 a month on Self-Service, 20 on Growth and 30 on Managed. Each visit is one Standard Visit: 1 post or reel plus 2 stories, live within 48 hours, with tracked delivery proof on your dashboard.
The price
$299 a month, flat, with no contracts, as published in October 2026.
The same $299 a month gets you in on Self-Service. Growth is $599 a month and Managed is $999 a month, each with a 3-month minimum, then month to month. The minimum exists because local creator marketing compounds over a season; one month is a coupon.
Who it is for
Restaurants, and the creators who post about food: Mustard describes its network as vetted local food influencers.
Restaurants and bars, plus salons and barbershops, med spas, fitness studios, retail, attractions and local services. One roster per city, across categories, because the creator who posts your tacos on Monday can post a salon on Friday.
The tracking
Mustard tracks posts and reports engagement and reach in a live dashboard, with unique promo codes and tracking links per creator.
SIGNL verifies the visit itself: a single-use code redeemed inside the SIGNL app, never at your register, starts the 48-hour posting clock. Creators are held to a published no-show and late-post policy with real consequences, enforced automatically, and every creator's delivery record is on their profile for you to see before you choose.
The audience
Mustard matches your restaurant with creators from its network, which the company sizes at over 12,000 vetted local food influencers across 27 cities.
Before you send a single offer, every SIGNL creator profile shows the share of that creator's followers who live in your market, labeled by source: Verified by Instagram, or Self-reported. After the post, its performance numbers (views and likes) are collected and shown on your dashboard.
The human option
The published base plan is fully automated, with no dedicated human support. Upgraded plans with human help exist, at prices Mustard does not publish.
SIGNL's Managed tier is $999 a month, published: a human coordinator picks creators, books visits, chases posts and reports to you monthly.

Choose Mustard if you run a restaurant, 3 automated visits a month is the right volume, and you want software only, with no contract and no humans.

Choose SIGNL if you want more visits per month, a business type beyond restaurants, local audience percentages before you choose, a tracked visit standard with published enforcement, and a human tier at a published price.

Optional · Paid visits

When you want one specific creator

Comps are the default rail on SIGNL. When a business wants a particular creator who charges, SIGNL runs that as a paid visit at the creator's own rate, under the same tracked standard.

/ 01

Comp is the default

Standard visits run on the comp you offer, provided at your cost. Comp-only is always fine, forever; no business on SIGNL is ever required to pay a creator cash.

/ 02

Book a name, at their rate

Creators can list a paid rate on their profile, with a $50 floor. Book one and you pay their rate plus 3 percent; the creator keeps 97 percent of their rate. The total fee is 6 percent, split evenly between you and the creator.

/ 03

Held until the post is live

SIGNL holds the payment and releases it when the creator's post is live, under the same 48-hour tracked standard as a comp visit. No post, no release; a no-show refunds instead.

Comparison FAQ

The questions people actually ask

What is the best Mustard alternative?

For a restaurant that wants more than 3 visits a month, or for any local business that is not a restaurant, SIGNL is the direct alternative: the same visit-and-post model at the same $299 a month entry price, with up to 8 visits on Self-Service, published tiers up to a human Managed plan at $999 a month, and per-creator local audience data on every profile. If you run a restaurant and 3 automated visits a month with no contract is exactly right, Mustard itself is a fair pick.

Mustard vs SIGNL: what is the difference?

Mustard is automated software for restaurants: $299 a month as published, 3 influencer matches and hosted visits included each month, no contracts, and a network the company describes as over 12,000 vetted local food influencers across 27 cities in the US and Canada. SIGNL is a local roster system for every walk-in business type: $299, $599 or $999 a month by creator visits (up to 8, 20 or 30 a month), a tracked Standard Visit of 1 post or reel plus 2 stories within 48 hours, local audience share on every creator profile, and an optional paid lane for booking specific creators at their own rate.

How much does SIGNL cost compared with Mustard?

Both start at $299 a month, as published in October 2026. The difference is what the $299 buys. Mustard includes 3 influencer matches and hosted visits a month, with no contracts. SIGNL Self-Service includes up to 8 creator visits a month, about $37 per visit, with a 3-month minimum and then month to month. SIGNL also publishes Growth at $599 a month (up to 20 visits) and Managed at $999 a month (up to 30 visits, with a human coordinator). On both platforms, the comp itself is the business's own product, provided at its cost, not retail.

Does SIGNL work for businesses that are not restaurants?

Yes. SIGNL's roster is built per city across categories: restaurants and bars, salons and barbershops, med spas and aesthetics, fitness studios, retail shops, attractions and local services. Mustard is built for restaurants and food creators. If your customers walk through your door, your category exists on SIGNL.

Can I pay a specific creator instead of offering a comp?

Yes, on SIGNL that is optional and always your choice. Creators may list a paid rate on their profile. Booking one costs their rate plus 3 percent on your side, and the creator keeps 97 percent of their rate, so the total fee is 6 percent, split evenly. SIGNL holds the payment until the tracked post is live. Comp-only is always fine, forever.

What happens if a creator does not post?

Every SIGNL visit runs on a tracked standard: 1 post or reel plus 2 stories, live within 48 hours of the visit. Creators are held to a published no-show and late-post policy with real consequences, enforced automatically, and businesses can see every creator's delivery and on-time record before they choose. A visit that never gets its post is never counted as delivered.
