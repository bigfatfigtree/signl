The math

Why local beats big, in numbers

A local business's customers live within about ten miles of its door. National brands shop by follower count because they ship products anywhere. Local businesses shop by audience location, and that flips who wins.

Creator FAQ

Questions, answered straight

Can small creators really get brand deals?

On SIGNL, small is the point. Brand deals for small creators usually fail because national brands shop by follower count; local businesses shop by audience location. SIGNL tiers start at 500 followers (Neighbors), anyone can apply at any follower count, and a creator with 1,000 followers whose audience lives in one city is exactly who local businesses want to book.

Do I need to call myself an influencer?

No. The Neighbors tier is built for everyday local people: parents, students, regulars, the friend everyone asks where to eat. If people who live near you follow you and trust your taste, you are what local businesses are looking for.

Is 500 followers a hard minimum?

No. Anyone can apply at any follower count, including under 500. Your tier is set by your follower count at sign-up and moves up as you grow. What matters most is that your audience is actually local and actually engages.

How fast can I get my first comp?

Founding markets open in waves over the next 30 to 90 days as rosters fill, and founding creators are reviewed and see offers in sign-up order. The earlier you sign up, the earlier your first claim. Once your market is open, offers appear as local businesses post them.

What do I have to post for a deal?

A story, post or reel about the visit, live within 48 hours, in your own voice, with a clear disclosure that the visit was comped (for example #gifted). Honest only: no business can require a positive post, ever.

Can I get paid, or is it only comps?

Both paths are open. Standard deals pay in comps: the meal, service or experience. Any creator can also set a paid rate on their profile, at any follower count. When a business books you directly at your rate, you keep 97 percent of it, because SIGNL takes only 3 percent of the creator's rate. Grow into the Headliners band (15,000+) and you are also first in line for paid cash campaigns on top of comps.
