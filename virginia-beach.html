For creators: the deal, up front

SIGNL is free for creators. Always. Businesses pay the platform. You never pay to join, to list, or to get booked.

Comp visits are the core. Paid bookings sit on top: set your own rate, the business pays it, and the platform fee is 6 percent total, split evenly, so you keep 97 percent of your rate. Paid bookings are held by SIGNL until your post is live. Headliners (15,000+ followers) are also first in line for paid hero campaigns when businesses run them.

$299 a month for Self-Service (up to 8 creator visits a month, about $37 per creator visit), $599 a month for Growth (up to 20 creator visits a month, about $30 per creator visit) or $999 a month for Managed (up to 30 creator visits a month), with a 3-month minimum and then month to month. The Visit Guarantee

If a booked creator does not show up or does not post, that visit does not count against your monthly visits. It is replaced.

How content rights work: on a comp visit, you can share the creator's post natively with the creator credited, and nothing more: no downloads, no reposting the file, no ads. On a paid visit, you can also repost the content on your own channels and run it as an ad for 6 months from the post date. The creator always owns the content. Rights follow the visit, never the plan.
