How it works

Three steps. No ad account. No agency retainer.

/ 01

Booked in minutes

You post a comp offer: dinner for two, a free cut and color, a detailing package. You set the value and how many visits you want. Local creators claim it and come in like real customers, because they are.

/ 02

Posted within 48 hours

Every visit carries a hard rule: post within 48 hours, with proper FTC disclosure, or lose your roster spot. No scripts, no staged shoots. Their own voice, to people who actually live here.

/ 03

Counted at the register

Posts are tracked in your dashboard alongside offer redemptions, so you see exactly what you got. The SIGNL ROAS Score rolls the visit, the post, and the register into one number a business can plan on.

Local on purpose

National platforms sell reach. We sell regulars.

Your next hundred customers live within about ten miles of your door. A national audience does not, and no follower count changes that. SIGNL builds one roster per city, so every post lands in feeds close enough to walk in, come back next week, and bring a friend.

Why SIGNL

Built for local. Different on purpose.

Influencer platforms were built for national brands with ad teams. Voucher apps were built for discounts. SIGNL was built for businesses that live on word of mouth, and the differences are structural, not cosmetic.

/ 01

You see the local number

Every creator profile shows what share of that creator’s followers lives in your market, with the source printed next to it: Verified by Instagram, or Self-reported. If the data is not solid yet, the profile says “not enough data yet” instead of guessing. Platforms built for national brands bury audience geography in expensive analytics tiers. We put the number on the profile, where you actually decide.

/ 02

Your register stays out of it

Comps redeem inside the SIGNL app at the counter, on a code tied to that one visit. Your staff never validate a voucher at the point of sale, never learn a second system in the middle of a rush, and your POS is never touched.

/ 03

A guarantee with teeth

We guarantee tracked deliverables. An accepted visit owes a disclosed post within 48 hours, and if it does not land, the visit is replaced and never counts against your plan. What we will never guarantee is a headcount or a glowing review. Anyone promising either one is selling you a guess.

/ 05

Neighbors are on the roster

Our roster starts at 500 followers. A regular whose 900 followers all live within ten miles of your door is not a small influencer. She is the most trusted voice in the neighborhood, and the big platforms skip her entirely.
