How it works

Three steps. No ad account. No agency retainer.

/ 01

You post a comp offer

List what you're comping — dinner for two, a free cut and color, a detailing package. You set the value and how many visits you want. The comp is your only cost beyond the subscription; creators are never on anyone's payroll.

/ 02

Creators pick it & visit

Local creators and everyday people from the roster claim your offer and come in like real customers — because they are. They experience the business, not a script.

/ 03

Post live in 48 hours

Every visit carries a hard rule: post within 48 hours, with proper FTC disclosure, or lose your roster spot. Posts are tracked in your dashboard alongside offer redemptions, so you see exactly what you got.

Why SIGNL

Built for local. Different on purpose.

Influencer platforms were built for national brands with ad teams. Voucher apps were built for discounts. SIGNL was built for businesses that live on word of mouth, and the differences are structural, not cosmetic.

/ 01

You see the local number

Every creator profile shows what share of that creator's followers lives in your market, with the source printed next to it: Verified by Instagram, or Self-reported. If the data is not solid yet, the profile says "not enough data yet" instead of guessing. Platforms built for national brands bury audience geography in expensive analytics tiers. We put the number on the profile, where you actually decide.

/ 02

Your register stays out of it

Comps redeem inside the SIGNL app at the counter, on a code tied to that one visit. Your staff never validate a voucher at the point of sale, never learn a second system in the middle of a rush, and your POS is never touched.

/ 03

A guarantee with teeth

We guarantee tracked deliverables. An accepted visit owes a disclosed post within 48 hours, and if it does not land, the visit is replaced and never counts against your plan. What we will never guarantee is a headcount or a glowing review. Anyone promising either one is selling you a guess.

/ 04

Run it, or we run it

Self-Service at $299/mo is fully automated: browse the roster, send offers, track posts. Growth at $599/mo adds up to 20 visits a month plus repost and ad rights while your account is active. Managed at $999/mo adds a human coordinator who picks creators, books the visits, chases the posts and writes your monthly report. Same roster, same rules. You choose how much of it you touch.

/ 05

Neighbors are on the roster

Our roster starts at 500 followers. A regular whose 900 followers all live within ten miles of your door is not a small influencer. She is the most trusted voice in the neighborhood, and the big platforms skip her entirely.

/ 06

Every post becomes an asset

Every tracked post lands in your content library with clear reuse rights, ready for your own social feeds, ads and website. The visit ends. The content keeps working.

/ 07

The full profile, before you book

You never choose from a follower count alone. Every creator profile shows photos, a bio, the jobs they have already delivered on SIGNL, their local audience share with its source, and a Local Impact Score that weighs local reach, engagement and reliability.
