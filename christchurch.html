For creators: the deal, up front

Magpie is free for creators. Always. Businesses pay the platform. You never pay to join, to list, or to get booked.

Comp visits are the core. Paid bookings sit on top: set your own rate from a NZ$90 floor, the business pays it, and the platform fee is 6 percent total, split evenly, so you keep 97 percent of your rate. Paid bookings are held by Magpie until your post is live. Headliners (15,000+ followers) are also first in line for featured paid campaigns when businesses run them.

Why Christchurch, why now

Christchurch eats by neighbourhood

The rebuild is the story

Christchurch has spent a decade opening new rooms: markets, laneways and restaurants where empty lots used to be. New openings are this city's favourite news, and founding creators get first pick of every one.

Market town at heart

Riverside Market feeds the central city daily, and weekend farmers markets run from Riccarton to Lyttelton. Cantabrians buy local on principle, and they follow the people who post where local is best.

Neighbourhoods over the centre

Visitors do the tram loop. Locals eat in Merivale, drink in Addington, get their hair cut in Riccarton and drive to Sumner when the sun is out. Magpie creators post that second city, the one residents trust for where to go.

City, port and plains

From Lyttelton over the Port Hills to Akaroa at the end of the peninsula, and out to Rangiora, Rolleston and Lincoln on the plains, small rooms keep opening, and each one survives on local word of mouth.

For Christchurch businesses

Christchurch businesses: be the room locals post about

Cantabrians choose the neighbourhood café over the famous name, and they choose it because someone they follow ate there. Founding businesses get that someone: real local creators, comped at your cost, posting honestly to the followers who fill your tables.

Ads reach everyone except the people who live nearby. Magpie puts Christchurch creators who live here inside your business, posting to neighbors who can walk in this week.

/ 01

Join the founding business list

Apply in a few minutes. Your category spot in your trade area is held in application order, and nothing is charged today.

/ 02

Founding pricing, held for you

NZ$349 a month for Self-Service (up to 5 creator visits a month, about NZ$70 per creator visit), NZ$619 a month for Growth (up to 10 creator visits a month, about NZ$62 per creator visit) or NZ$1,239 a month for Managed (up to 20 creator visits a month), with a 3-month minimum and then month to month. Founding businesses get NZ$175 off the first month, and all prices are plus GST. Visits you don't use roll into the next month, then expire. The only other cost is the comp the business chooses to offer, provided at its cost, not retail. Founding businesses keep founding pricing while they remain subscribed, and the plan begins when the Christchurch market opens.

/ 03

Activate when Christchurch opens

Pick creators from the founding roster, offer a comp at your cost, and get honest, tracked posts delivered in 48 hours* of each visit.

*Creators have up to one week to post. The goal is to post within 48 hours of the visit, and hitting it earns their Momentum. If a week passes with no post, the visit is replaced and does not count against your plan.

The Visit Guarantee

If a booked creator does not show up or does not post, that visit does not count against your monthly visits. It is replaced.

How content rights work: on a comp visit, you can share the creator's post natively with the creator credited, and nothing more: no downloads, no reposting the file, no ads. On a paid visit, you can also repost the content on your own channels and run it as an ad for 90 days under the standard license included in the booking. The creator always owns the content. Rights follow the visit, never the plan.
