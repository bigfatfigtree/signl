The comp rules, read these

Simple deal, enforced fairly

The comp is the pay

A free meal or service is your compensation. Creators are never on a payroll, businesses supply the comp, you supply the post.

48-hour posting rule

Your post must be live within 48 hours of the visit. Late or missing posts are tracked against your roster standing.

FTC disclosure is required

Every post must clearly disclose the comp (e.g. #gifted / "meal provided by…"). No exceptions, it protects you and the business.

No-shows lose their roster spot

Claim it, show up. A no-show without notice, or a visit with no post, removes you from the roster. Businesses plan around your visit.

Honest posts only

Nobody can require a positive review. Post your real take. Businesses that pressure creators get removed, your credibility is the whole product.

Top tier gets paid, too

Reach 15,000 or more stacked followers and paid cash campaigns open up: first in line, your own listed rate, and you keep 97 percent of it. Any creator can also set a paid rate for direct bookings. Comp is the everyday lane here, never the only one.

Creator FAQ

Questions, answered straight

Does it cost anything to join SIGNL as a creator?

No. SIGNL is free for creators. Always. Businesses pay the platform. You never pay to join, to list, or to get booked. Your compensation on standard visits is the comp itself: the meal, service or experience the business provides.

What does SIGNL charge on paid bookings?

Joining SIGNL and comped visits are always free for creators. On paid bookings the platform fee is 6 percent total, split evenly, so creators keep 97 percent of their rate. Paid bookings are held by SIGNL until the post is live.

How do creators get paid on SIGNL?

On standard visits, the comp is the pay: a free meal, service or experience in exchange for an honest post. Headliners (15,000+ followers) are also eligible for paid hero campaigns when businesses run them. Any creator, at any tier, can also set a paid rate on their profile. When a business books you directly at your rate, you keep 97 percent of it, because SIGNL takes only 3 percent of the creator's rate.

Do I have to post something positive?

Never. Honest posts only, that is the entire product. Businesses cannot require a positive post or review, and a business that pressures a creator is removed from the platform. Post your real experience, in your own voice.

What do I have to post, and how fast?

1 post or reel plus 2 stories about the visit, live within 48 hours of the visit, with a clear disclosure that it was comped (for example “comped” or #gifted), as FTC guidelines require. If a claimed visit gets no post, the business gets a replacement visit and the creator's roster standing takes the hit; repeat no-shows lose their spot.

I have fewer than 500 followers. Can I still apply?

Yes, any follower count can apply. Your tier (Neighbors 500–2,000, Locals 2,000–15,000, Headliners 15,000+) is set by your follower count and rises as you grow. A small, genuinely local audience beats a big, scattered one.

My city is not in the rollout. Can I still join SIGNL?

Yes. SIGNL's founding markets now number 37 across 8 countries, and founding rosters are forming in all of them now. In any other city, apply with your home city and you start its list: when your city opens, its early applicants become its founding roster. Cities are picked by signup counts.

How do I get comped meals for posting?

Apply for your city's roster; joining is free. Once you are on it, local businesses post comp offers: a meal for two, a service, an experience. You claim the ones you want, visit like a real customer, and post honestly about it within 48 hours, with a clear disclosure that the visit was comped. On standard visits, the comp itself is the pay.

Can small creators get brand deals?

On SIGNL, small is the point. Brand deals for small creators usually fail because national brands shop by follower count; local businesses shop by audience location. SIGNL tiers start at 500 followers (Neighbors), anyone can apply at any follower count, and a creator with 1,000 followers whose audience lives in one city is exactly who local businesses want to book.

How do I become a local creator?

Apply with your home city and your handle, and fill in your profile yourself: your photo, bio and follower counts. When your city opens, claim offers, show up, and post within 48 hours. Every delivered post grows your reliability record and your Local Impact Score, and that record is what gets you picked first.

Do restaurants really give free food for posts?

Yes. That is the comp model: the business offers a free meal or service, and in exchange you post your honest experience to your own audience. Two rules keep it real: you disclose that the visit was comped, as FTC guidelines require (for example #gifted), and the post has to be your honest take. Businesses cannot buy a positive post, and SIGNL removes any that try.

Is SIGNL a UGC creator program?

Partly, with a difference. In a typical UGC creator program you produce content and hand it to a brand to post on its own channels. On SIGNL you post to your own audience as a local creator, which is why local businesses value it: your followers trust you. Delivered posts are also saved to the business's content library. On a comp visit the business can share your post natively and nothing more; repost rights and ad use come only with paid visits, and ads run for 6 months from the post date at most. You always own your content.

How long does review take?

Every founding application gets a decision or a clear status within 2 business days. Applications are reviewed by a person, in sign-up order. If a decision needs more time, you still get a clear status inside that window.
