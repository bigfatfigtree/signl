The comp rules — read these

Simple deal, enforced fairly

  • The comp is the pay A free meal or service is your compensation. Creators are never on a payroll — businesses supply the comp, you supply the post.
  • 48-hour posting rule Your post must be live within 48 hours of the visit. Late or missing posts are tracked against your roster standing.
  • FTC disclosure is required Every post must clearly disclose the comp (e.g. #gifted / "meal provided by…"). No exceptions — it protects you and the business.
  • No-shows lose their roster spot Claim it, show up. A no-show without notice — or a visit with no post — removes you from the roster. Businesses plan around your visit.
  • Honest posts only Nobody can require a positive review. Post your real take. Businesses that pressure creators get removed — your credibility is the whole product.
