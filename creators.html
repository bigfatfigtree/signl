Creator FAQ

Questions, answered straight

Does it cost anything to join SIGNL as a creator?

No. Joining and staying on the roster is free, and creators are never charged fees. Your compensation on standard visits is the comp itself — the meal, service or experience the business provides.

How do creators get paid on SIGNL?

On standard visits, the comp is the pay: a free meal, service or experience in exchange for an honest post. Creators in the top tier (15,000+ followers) are also eligible for paid hero campaigns when businesses run them.

Do I have to post something positive?

Never. Honest posts only — that is the entire product. Businesses cannot require a positive post or review, and a business that pressures a creator is removed from the platform. Post your real experience, in your own voice.

What do I have to post, and how fast?

A story, post or reel about the visit, live within 48 hours of the visit, with a clear disclosure that it was comped (for example “comped” or #gifted), as FTC guidelines require. If a claimed visit gets no post, the creator loses their roster spot.

I have fewer than 500 followers. Can I still apply?

Yes — any follower count can apply. Your tier (Neighbors 500–2,000, Locals 2,000–15,000, Creators 15,000+) is set by your follower count and rises as you grow. A small, genuinely local audience beats a big, scattered one.

I'm not in Hampton Roads. Can I still join SIGNL?

Yes. SIGNL launches city by city, and Hampton Roads, Virginia is City #1. Apply with your home city and you are first on its list: when your city opens, its early applicants become its founding roster. Cities are picked by signup counts.
