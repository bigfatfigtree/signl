For service providers: the deal, up front

Magpie is free for creators. Always. Businesses pay the platform. You never pay to join, to list, or to get booked.

How it works

How Magpie works for you

/ 01

List your services and your rate

Claim your founding spot free in the app and finish your page: the services you sell, the work you show, and the rate you charge. Your rate is public on your page, so a business sees your price before it books you. Your page is live and on Google the day you finish it.

/ 02

A business books you to make content

When your market opens, businesses book you through Magpie to make content: a shoot, a reel, coverage of an opening or an event. The booking states the deliverables and timing up front, in terms you accept before the work starts. The business pays up front and Magpie holds the money.

/ 03

Deliver, and the money releases

Deliver the work and Magpie releases the payment to you. You keep 97 percent of your rate. No invoices, no chasing, and no Net 30, 60 or 90 day terms.

Creator FAQ

Questions, answered straight

How is this different from finding my own clients?

Magpie is not lead generation for your own practice. A business books you through Magpie, at the rate listed on your page, and pays through Magpie. You keep 97 percent of your rate. There is no list of leads to chase and no pitch to write.

Do I have to take comped visits?

No. Paid bookings only is completely fine. Set your page to paid work and businesses hire you to make content at your listed rate. Nobody builds standing by taking comps they do not want, and skipping comps never counts against you.

What do businesses hire me to make?

Content for the business: photos, short video, reels and coverage of openings, menus, products and events. The booking states the deliverables and timing up front, in the terms you accept before the work starts.

What rights does the business get?

A paid booking includes one standard license: the business can repost your work and run it as an ad for 90 days. Anything beyond that is a separate licensing request at your own listed fee, approved by you per use. Nothing is perpetual, ever. You own your work.

How and when do I get paid?

The business pays up front and Magpie holds the money until the work is delivered, then releases it to you. You keep 97 percent of your rate. No invoices, no chasing, and no Net 30, 60 or 90 day terms.

Does it cost anything to join, and who can join?

No. Magpie is free for creators, including service providers. Businesses pay the platform. You need 500 total followers across your platforms to join, with your counts stacked across Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, X and LinkedIn.
