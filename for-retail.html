How it works

How SIGNL works here

/ 01

Post the comp

Offer a product or a store credit with a ceiling you set: the new arrival, the seasonal drop, the piece that photographs best. You set the value and how many visits you want this month, and the comp costs you your cost, not its retail price.

/ 02

Creators shop like real customers

Local creators claim the offer, book through the app and redeem the comp inside the app on a single-use code. Your register and your point of sale are never touched, and the visit looks like what it is: a local shopping at your store.

/ 03

The post lands in 48 hours*

A tracked Standard Visit about the real visit: 1 post or reel plus 1 story that shares the post and what they picked, with honest words and full disclosure. If a week passes with no post, the visit is replaced and does not count against your plan.

*Creators have up to one week to post. 48 hours is the standard they are held to, and hitting it earns their Momentum. If a week passes with no post, the visit is replaced and does not count against your plan.

Honest FAQ

Questions, answered straight

Does influencer marketing work for retail stores and boutiques?

Yes, because local retail runs on discovery and word of mouth. A national ad reaches people who will never drive to your store; a local creator's post reaches their actual neighbors. Creators shop like real customers, post honestly within 48 hours, and every post is tracked, so you can see exactly which visits your plan produced.

What does a creator get comped at a store?

A product you choose, or a store credit with a ceiling you set: a new arrival, a seasonal piece, a book, a plant. You set the comp and its ceiling on every offer, and creators opt in knowing exactly what it is. The comp costs you your cost, not its retail price, and SIGNL never touches comp money: no pass-through, no markup, and no per-creator cash fees on standard visits.

How much does it cost?

Published pricing: $199 a month for Self-Service (up to 5 creator visits a month, about $40 per creator visit), $349 a month for Growth (up to 10 creator visits a month, about $35 per creator visit) or $699 a month for Managed (up to 20 creator visits a month), with a 3-month minimum, then month to month. Your only other cost is the comp you choose to offer, provided at your cost, not retail. There are no per-creator cash fees on standard visits.

What if a creator does not post?

Creators post to a 48-hour standard, and on-time posts earn them Momentum. If no post has appeared within a week of the visit, you get a replacement visit and the slot does not count against your cap. Creators who no-show or do not post lose standing, and repeat offenders are removed from the roster entirely.

What is the Visit Guarantee?

If a booked creator does not show up or does not post, that visit does not count against your monthly visits. It is replaced.

What is the refund policy?

Paid bookings are held by SIGNL until the post is live. If the visit does not happen or the post never lands, the business is refunded in full automatically. If we cannot deliver the service, including a founding market that does not open, founding businesses receive a full refund of what they paid for that market. Plans run month to month after the 3 month minimum. Cancelling stops future billing. Visits already delivered are not refundable.
