How it works

The Location Drop

/ 01

Offer a full meal at the window

Comp a full meal at the window at the stop you want packed: the new brewery night, the slow weekday lunch corner. You set the comp and its ceiling on every offer, and creators opt in knowing exactly what it is. The comp costs you your cost, not its menu price.

/ 02

Creators visit like real guests

Local creators claim the offer, show up at the stop you posted, and redeem the comp inside the app on a single-use code. Your register and your point of sale are never touched, and the visit reads like any other order at your window.

/ 03

The post lands in 48 hours*

A tracked Standard Visit about the real visit: 1 post or reel plus 1 story that shares the post, with your location and hours in it, honestly told and fully disclosed. If a week passes with no post, the visit is replaced and does not count against your plan.

*Creators have up to one week to post. The goal is to post within 48 hours of the visit, and hitting it earns their Momentum. If a week passes with no post, the visit is replaced and does not count against your plan.

The Visit Guarantee

A creator who does not show up, or shows up and never posts, does not burn your visit. It is replaced, and the slot does not count against your plan. Creators who no-show or do not post lose standing, and repeat offenders come off the roster.

How Magpie works here

/ 01

Local reach, printed before you choose

Every creator is ranked by local audience share, the share of their followers who live in your market, ahead of follower count. A smaller account whose people live here outranks a big account whose audience lives somewhere else.

/ 02

One defined deliverable

One post, plus one story that shares it. The terms sit on one card both sides confirm before the visit: what posts, by when, one round of edits, what rights, when money moves. No vague briefs, no endless revisions.

/ 03

Comp it, or book it paid

Comp the visit, or book a creator paid at their own listed rate. On paid bookings the creator keeps 97 percent, and the money is held safely until the post is live, then paid the day it posts.

/ 04

Proof, counted three ways

Proof is counted, not promised: creators checked in, posts live, and offers redeemed. Codes are counted at your register, on pickup orders and on delivery orders, and staff log them on one screen with nothing installed at your point of sale.

The proof

Proof, not promises

After a visit, the creator shares their creator code. Guests who use that code at your register are tallied back to the visit, so your Magpie ROAS Score starts from real tracked register spend, a floor, and adds modeled media value, labeled as modeled.

Honest FAQ

Questions, answered straight

What food truck marketing ideas actually build a line?

The ones tied to where you are parked today. A local creator posts your location drop, your menu and your hours to followers who live in your market, and their code is counted when those followers order. Magpie ranks creators by local audience share, the share of their followers who live nearby, ahead of follower count, so the post reaches people who can actually get to your window.

How does this work when my truck moves every day?

You post your stops like you always do, and creator visits are booked against the stop you choose: a regular corner, a brewery night, an event. The creator redeems the comp in the app at that stop, and the post carries that location while it is still useful, because the goal is a post within 48 hours of the visit.

What does the comp actually cost my truck?

Whatever you decide to offer: a full meal at the window is the standard play. You set the comp and its ceiling on every offer, and creators opt in knowing exactly what it is. The comp costs you your cost, not its menu price: the ingredients in one meal, not the price on the board. The comp is your own product, provided by you, and Magpie never touches comp money: no pass-through, no markup, and no per-creator cash fees on standard visits.

How much does it cost?

Published founding pricing: $199 a month for Self-Service (up to 5 creator visits a month, about $40 per visit), $349 a month for Growth (up to 10 visits, about $35 per visit), or $699 a month for Managed (up to 20 visits). Signing up includes $100 off each of your first 3 months, and your founding rate is held while your plan stays active. Plans carry a 3-month minimum once active, then run month to month. Your only other cost is the comp you choose to offer, provided at your cost, not retail. There are no per-creator cash fees on standard visits.

When do I pay?

Not today. Signing up locks your founding price and holds your category spot in application order. Businesses sign up when we are ready to launch, market by market as each founding roster completes. Your plan, and your billing, begin when your market activates. Nothing is charged before that.

What if a creator does not show up, or never posts?

That is what the Visit Guarantee is for. The goal is for creators to post within 48 hours of the visit, and on-time posts earn them Momentum. If no post has appeared within a week of the visit, the visit is replaced and does not count against your plan. Creators who no-show or do not post lose standing, and repeat offenders are removed from the roster entirely.

How do I know it worked?

Every visit can carry an offer with a code your staff logs on one screen, and redemptions are counted wherever they land: in store, on pickup orders, on delivery orders. Your results screen shows tracked spend as a floor, worded as at least this much, with the counting method printed under it, and a return code counts how many of those customers came back within 30 days. You also set one baseline number at signup, your usual crowd on the night in question, so results read against your own normal, not a guess.
